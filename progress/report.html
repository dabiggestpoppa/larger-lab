🔍 Key Finding

Cross-domain connection discovered: The research agent found "Fractional Brownian Motions, Fractional Noises and Applications" (7,678 citations, confidence 0.76) as a bridge between PINNs and volatility trading.

Why this matters: Fractional Brownian motion (fBm) is used in both domains:
  • PINNs: fBm appears in stochastic PDEs that PINNs solve — it models anomalous diffusion and memory effects in physical systems
  • Volatility trading: fBm is used in financial modeling to capture long-range dependence in asset returns, which directly maps to volatility clustering and persistence
This is a genuine latent connection that would be difficult to find through simple keyword search.
