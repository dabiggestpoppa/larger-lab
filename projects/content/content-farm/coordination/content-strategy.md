## 5 Content Verticals (Testing Phase)

### Vertical 1: AI Art Showcase
- **Concept:** "Best AI art of the week" — curated carousels and slideshows
- **Target Platform:** TikTok (primary), Instagram (secondary)
- **Posting Frequency:** 3x/week per platform
- **Content Format:** 
  - TikTok: Beat-synced image sequences (6-12 images), 15-30 seconds
  - Instagram: Carousel posts (10 slides), clean grid aesthetic
- **Hashtag Strategy:** #aiart #aiartcommunity #digitalart #midjourney #aiartist #generativeart #aiartwork #artificialintelligence #aiartdaily #digitalartist #aiartgallery #stablediffusion #aiartlovers #ai #art #aiartgenerator
- **Hook Formula:** "The AI art community is going crazy for these" / "I found the best AI art so you don't have to"
- **CTA:** "Follow for more AI art daily" / "Which one is your favorite? Comment below"

### Vertical 2: Prompt Breakdowns
- **Concept:** "How this image was made" — educational content showing prompts and generation process
- **Target Platform:** TikTok (primary), X threads (secondary)
- **Posting Frequency:** 2x/week per platform
- **Content Format:**
  - TikTok: Process reveal videos (15-30s), show prompt → iterations → final
  - X: Thread format with prompt in thread replies
- **Hashtag Strategy:** #promptengineering #aiprompt #midjourney #stablediffusion #promptdesign #aiarttips #aitutorial #learnai #aiprompting #stablediffusionprompts #aiarttutorial #generativeart #aitools #promptshare #aiarthelp
- **Hook Formula:** "This prompt generates insane results every time" / "3 prompt tweaks that instantly improve your AI art"
- **CTA:** "Save this prompt for later" / "Try it and tag me in your results"

### Vertical 3: Model Reviews & Comparisons
- **Concept:** "I tested [Model X] for 30 days" — model showcases and A/B comparisons
- **Target Platform:** TikTok (primary), Instagram (secondary)
- **Posting Frequency:** 2x/week per platform
- **Content Format:**
  - TikTok: Side-by-side comparison videos, "Model A vs Model B"
  - Instagram: Carousel with comparison grids
- **Hashtag Strategy:** #aimodel #stablediffusion #midjourney #civitai #aimodels #modelcomparison #sdxl #aiarttools #generativeai #aiartcommunity #newmodel #aimodelreview #stablediffusionmodels #civitaicommunity #aitools
- **Hook Formula:** "I tested every popular AI model so you don't have to" / "This new AI model changes everything"
- **CTA:** "Which style do you prefer? 1 or 2?" / "Follow for more AI model reviews"

### Vertical 4: Trending Reactions & Themed Series
- **Concept:** "AI versions of [trending topic]" — ride cultural waves with AI interpretations
- **Target Platform:** TikTok (primary), X (secondary)
- **Posting Frequency:** 2-3x/week (opportunistic, tied to trends)
- **Content Format:**
  - TikTok: Fast-cut transformation videos synced to trending sounds
  - X: Single image posts with punchy captions
- **Hashtag Strategy:** Mix trending hashtags with evergreen AI tags. Rotate based on what's trending.
  - Base: #aiart #trending #viral #fyp #foryoupage
  - Add 2-3 trending tags per post
- **Hook Formula:** "Everyone's talking about [X], so I asked AI to show us" / "The AI community can't stop generating this"
- **CTA:** "What should I generate next?" / "Share this with someone who needs to see it"

### Vertical 5: Before/After & Transformations
- **Concept:** Raw generation vs polished, sketch to masterpiece, "AI glow-up" content
- **Target Platform:** TikTok (primary), Instagram (secondary)
- **Posting Frequency:** 2x/week per platform
- **Content Format:**
  - TikTok: Split-screen or quick-cut before/after (10-20s)
  - Instagram: Carousel (before → process → after)
- **Hashtag Strategy:** #beforeandafter #aitransformation #aiart #digitalart #aiartwork #transformation #aiartcommunity #aiartist #generativeart #aiartdaily #aigenerated #aiartgallery #digitaltransformation #aiartlovers #aitutorial
- **Hook Formula:** "From stick figure to Hollywood concept art in 20 seconds" / "The AI glow-up is unreal"
- **CTA:** "Which version hits different?" / "Follow for more transformations"

## Vertical Testing Protocol

**Week 1-2:** Post all 5 verticals at planned frequency
**Week 3:** Analyze performance (views, engagement rate, follower growth per vertical)
**Week 4:** Double down on top 2 performers, reduce bottom 2, test 1 new angle
**Ongoing:** Monthly review, rotate underperformers, scale winners

---

## Branding Guidelines

- **Visual Identity:** Consistent watermark/logo on all images (bottom corner, semi-transparent)
- **Color Palette:** Dark backgrounds with neon accents (purple/cyan) — stands out in feeds
- **Series Naming:** Give each vertical a branded name (e.g., "Prompt Lab", "AI Spotlight", "Model Wars")
- **Voice:** Confident, slightly edgy, educational but entertaining. Not corporate. Not cringe.

## Compliance Notes

- **TikTok/IG:** SFW only. No explicit content. Label AI content per platform requirements.
- **X:** Soft NSFW OK with proper tagging. Full NSFW in DMs/pinned threads.
- **Reddit:** NSFW content in appropriate subreddits only. Follow each sub's rules.
- **All platforms:** Always label AI-generated content (TikTok requires it, +23% higher views for transparent AI content).
- **CivitAI content:** Check individual image licenses. Prefer Creative Commons / public domain for commercial use.
