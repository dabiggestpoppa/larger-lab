## Week 2 Schedule

### Content Rotation Rules
1. **Don't repeat the same vertical back-to-back** on the same platform
2. **Alternate formats:** Video → Carousel → Video → Story series
3. **Test one new format per day** (different hook, different sound, different editing style)
4. **Double down on what worked in Week 1** — check analytics Wednesday of Week 2
5. **Engagement posts** should be every other day, not daily (avoid fatigue)

### Week 2 Template (repeat structure with fresh content)
- Follow the same time slots as Week 1
- Rotate verticals so each gets 4-5 posts across the week
- Introduce **one new experimental format** (e.g., AI art + voiceover story, tutorial format)
- **Wednesday:** Mid-week analytics review — adjust remaining week based on data
- **Friday:** Launch a recurring series (e.g., "Model Monday" or "Prompt Friday")
