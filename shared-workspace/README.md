# SHARED WORKSPACE — BRAND BUILDOUT
# This is the team's living document. All agents read and update this.

## Mission
Build a crypto/Finance brand from scratch targeting people with zero trading knowledge.
The brand becomes the entry point. Trading is the product. Community is the moat.

## Brand Pillars
1. **Real Results** — Everything documented, nothing fabricated
2. **Accessible** — Financial literacy for people who've never opened a chart
3. **Underground** — Not another loud crypto influencer. Quiet confidence.
4. **Community-First** — The people ARE the brand

## Team
- **CEO** — Business, partnerships, monetization, strategy
- **Sage** — Vision, philosophy, long-range strategy, cultural trends
- **Content Team** — Social media, copywriting, community, engagement
- **Build Team** — Tools, bots, dashboards, automation, web
- **Librarian** — Coordination, knowledge management, workflow

## Target Audience (per MAD directive)
- PRIMARY: People with NO trading knowledge who want to make money
- SECONDARY: Established traders looking for community
- GROWTH: Retained traders become evangelists

## Rules
1. No fake numbers or guarantees
2. No "typical influencer" energy
3. Everything transparent and documented
4. Creative freedom within brand guardrails
5. Test everything, keep what works
