KEY INSIGHT — REGIME ACCURACY vs CLOSURE PROBABILITY:
IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII
  REGIME ACCURACY (97% for CONFIRMED):
    = "Did price move in the predicted direction of constraint resolution?"
    = VolBand >1.2 predicted expansion → did price expand beyond CRD?
    = Binary outcome: YES / NO
  CLOSURE PROBABILITY (84.1% for CONFIRMED):
    = "Did price close within the PRECISION ZONE of the exact target?"
    = Target was 72.5 pips ±3.0 pips → did close land in [69.5, 75.5]?
    = Continuous outcome: distance from target
  WHY THE GAP?
  Even when the regime direction is correct (97%), the EXACT target has variance:
    • Fib stall zones have 0.5-1.5p natural variance (per daily float data)
    • Spread/slippage/news spikes add noise
    • The 97% says "direction right" — the 84.1% says "target precise"
  PRACTICAL USE:
    Use Regime Accuracy for POSITION SIZE (CONFIRMED = full size)
    Use Closure Probability for TARGET ADJUSTMENT (84.1% = expect ±3p variance)

REGIME CLASSIFICATION LOGIC (12 PM EST)
IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII
  1. Classify Tier from Asian Range (as per manual tiers T1/T2/T3/SKIP)
  2. Calculate Current Range Delta (CRD):
     CRD = High(7PM → now) - Low(7PM → now)
  3. Calculate Volatility Band:
     VolBand = Tier Average Daily Range / CRD
  4. Classify Regime:
     VolBand > 1.2  → CONFIRMED   (97% direction accuracy — full size)
     VolBand 0.8-1.2 → NORMAL     (79% direction accuracy — standard size)
     VolBand < 0.8  → COMPRESSION (67% direction accuracy — reduce size)
  5. Calculate Closure Probability (weighted function):
     Tier weight:    35%  (T1=95pts, T2=90pts, T3=85pts)
     VolBand weight: 30%  (scaled 70-95 based on VolBand value)
     Time weight:    20%  (scaled to completion factor)
     Regime weight:  10%  (CONFIRMED=90, NORMAL=85, COMPRESSION=80)
     1hr Vol weight:  5%  (avg 1-hr range proxy for noise)
