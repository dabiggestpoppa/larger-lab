Executive Summary
This analysis decodes how daily and weekly price structures form monthly candles in oil markets through a systematic approach treating price
movements as engineered rather than random. The CEREBUS PRICE DELIVERY ENGINE model reveals consistent patterns in Fibonacci
sequences, time-based volatility windows, and price anchoring behavior.
Key Finding: The predominant Fibonacci flow pattern is 78.6→78.6→78.6, accounting for a significant portion of monthly candle
construction.
Key Finding: March shows significantly higher volatility (3.59 average range) than any other month, creating a clear seasonal pattern.
Key Finding: Day 1 has the highest frequency for both monthly highs (10.77%) and lows (13.85%), demonstrating the importance of month
beginnings.
Key Finding: Week 2 shows the highest reversal probability (20.68%), while Week 3 shows the greatest directional consistency.
