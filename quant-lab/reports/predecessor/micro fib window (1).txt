Key Analysis Insights
Pattern Effectiveness
The 15M timeframe shows the highest hit rates, with the AB-CD Bullish pattern (50% → -25%) achieving an impressive 84% hit rate.
AB-CD patterns generally outperform 3-Leg patterns in terms of hit rate, though 3-Leg patterns show stronger ILM continuation.
Bullish patterns tend to have higher hit rates than bearish patterns across timeframes.
The 4H timeframe shows the fastest pattern completion (3 bars average for AB-CD Bullish).
Session Analysis
The European/London session shows the highest overall effectiveness, with strong performance in both hit rates and ILM continuation.
The Asian session is particularly effective for 15M AB-CD Bullish patterns.
The NY session tends to favor bearish patterns in the 1H timeframe.
European session patterns show the strongest recursive behavior, often triggering higher timeframe activations.
Recursive Structure
Four patterns demonstrate timeframe-flipping properties, where lower timeframe completions trigger higher timeframe activations.
The strongest recursive chain is the 15M→1H→4H sequence starting with the AB-CD Bullish pattern.
1H patterns that trigger 4H activations show higher average ILM continuation rates (42-45%).
3-Leg patterns in 15M and 1H timeframes show significant ability to trigger higher timeframe patterns.
Fibonacci Sequence Insights
The 50% → -25% sequence shows the highest hit rate (84%) in the 15M timeframe.
3-Leg sequences with 78.6% as the middle leg show stronger ILM continuation rates.
The -100% extension level is common in 1H timeframe patterns, particularly for bearish patterns.
Sequences containing the 61.8% retracement level appear in 5 out of 10 patterns, showing its significance across timeframes.
