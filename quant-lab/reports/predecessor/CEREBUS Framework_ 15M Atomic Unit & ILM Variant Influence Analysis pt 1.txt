Executive Summary
This analysis examines the 15-minute (15M) timeframe as the fundamental "atomic unit" within the CEREBUS framework, with particular
focus on how various Internal Liquidity Move (ILM) variants influence market structure construction. Using data from the "2. Temporal
Pattern Confirmation" table, we investigate how Fibonacci sequence completions across different timeframes interact with various zone
types, demonstrating that ILM zones significantly accelerate high/low formation and increase completion probabilities compared to
regular zones.
Key Insight:
ILM zones demonstrate consistently faster high/low formation times (up to 34% faster) compared to regular zones across all
timeframes, with particularly significant acceleration in the 15M: -25% Fibonacci sequence, where the average time to high/low
formation decreases from 63 minutes in regular zones to just 47 minutes in ILM zones.
