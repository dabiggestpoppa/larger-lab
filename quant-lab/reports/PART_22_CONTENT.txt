TEST 2: RETEST TIMING — FLOAT CONTEXT
IMPORTANT CONTEXT ON RETEST TIMING:
  Monday 08:00 UTC = London Open → Price frequently tests the Asian band immediately.
  This is normal London session behavior — not the float signal.
  The TRUE float signal is whether the SUBSEQUENT Asian session (Tuesday 00:00-08:00 UTC)
  tests the Monday band. That requires price to have stayed outside through:
    → Mon London session  → Mon NY session  → Mon close  → Tue Asian open
  That is a genuine ~17-20h sustained directional move — a weekly constraint float.
  Tier 1-2 Float Rates:
    T1: 71.1% of Tue Asian sessions never test Monday range
    T2: 54.5% — both confirm the float as a real weekly structural phenomenon

KEY FINDING — TIMING:
  Counter-trend extreme (pullback in bull weeks, bounce in bear) forms at median 26-27h.
  Calendar: Mon Asian closes 08:00 UTC + 27h = Tue 11:00 UTC → Tuesday mid-morning European.
  Mon + Tue combined = 65-66% of all counter-trend extremes already formed.
  The "mid-week shift" in the charts is the CONTINUATION off that Mon/Tue extreme.
  Weekly extreme (full high/low): median ~58h → Thu Asian / early London.
  This is when the field completes its weekly constraint deficit resolution.

KEY FINDING — FLOAT WEEK STRUCTURE:
  In TRUE float weeks (Tue full day outside Mon range):
  63.0% of counter-trend extremes ALREADY FORMED ON MONDAY.
  The market made its pullback/bounce during Mon London/NY session — before Tue opened.
  Then price resumes the directional move with no Tue Asian retest.
  The mid-week test in the charts = price returning to Mon range after 48h+ of float.
  FLOAT vs NON-FLOAT timing difference:
    Float weeks:     counter-trend extreme at 7h median (Monday itself)
    Non-float weeks: counter-trend extreme at 30h median (Tue morning/afternoon)
  This bimodal behavior IS the float mechanism — float weeks exhaust their counter-trend
  on Monday, then run cleanly for 48-72h toward 161-261% weekly targets.

WEEKLY FLOAT OPERATIONAL FRAMEWORK
IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII
MONDAY PRE-SESSION (1:45 AM EST):
  1. Measure Monday Asian Range → Classify Tier (T1/T2/T3/T4)
  2. Mark Monday Asian HIGH and LOW as WEEKLY STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINT BOUNDARIES
  3. Float probability: T1=71% | T2=55% | T3=31% | T4=skip
  4. Mark fib extension states from Mon Asian range:
       Bull targets: 100%, 162%, 168%, 200%, 261% above Mon Asian High
       Bear targets: -68%, -100%, -161% below Mon Asian Low
MONDAY SESSION WATCH (3 AM – 12 PM EST):
  London open breaks out of Asian constraint band.
  Watch: does the counter-trend partial rebalancing happen on Monday?
    → 43-44% of counter-trend extremes form ON MONDAY itself
    → In float weeks: 63% form within first 12h (Mon session)
    → The low/high that forms on Monday = key constraint support/resistance all week
FLOAT CONFIRMATION (Tuesday Asian 00:00-08:00 UTC):
  If Tuesday Asian session does NOT overlap Monday Asian band → FLOAT CONFIRMED
  → Occurs 52.6% of Tier 1-2 weeks
  → Hold directional bias | Target 161-261% fib states
  → Expected weekly range: 5-9x Monday Asian range (tier dependent)
MID-WEEK RETEST EXPECTATION:
  Weekly constraint deficit fully resolves at median ~58h from Mon Asian close
  → Thursday Asian / early London
  → The mid-week charts show price returning to Mon range from outside
  → Either: bounces back (continuation, more common on 48h float)
  → Or:     breaks back through (less common, T3 weeks mostly)
