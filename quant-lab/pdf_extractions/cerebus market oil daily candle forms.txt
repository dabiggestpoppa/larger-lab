CEREBUS PARADIGM Conclusion
The oil price data reveals clear evidence that price is not randomly formed but follows precise mathematical relationships based on Fibonacci
sequences, range projections, and time cycles. The 72% Fibonacci level emerges as the central "reset logic" in the system, acting as the primary
decision point for price direction.
The recursive nature of patterns across timeframes confirms that price structure is hierarchical with smaller timeframe completions seeding larger
timeframe formations. This analysis supports the hypothesis that "price is not reacting — it is projected." The Fibonacci levels appear to function
as instructions rather than tools, with remarkable consistency in how price moves from one level to another, especially the dominant 72% → 100%
pathway.
Skip levels, time cycles, and measured moves demonstrate evidence of being predefined rather than emerging organically from market participant
behavior, suggesting an engineered system with deterministic yet adaptive rules.
