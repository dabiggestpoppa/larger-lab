1. Executive Summary

--- PAGE 1 ---
This manual defines the complete decision-tree protocol for trading Bitcoin (BTC)
and Ethereum (ETH) using the Friday Asian Session Range A model. Unlike
traditional forex models based on London Open volatility, this framework utilizes
the Asian session liquidity build (Thursday 22:00 - Friday 05:00 EST) as the
foundational range for projecting Friday's price action.
The core framework relies on Fibonacci extension targets (-25%, -50%, -100%,
-168%) derived from the 7-hour Range A size, validated across 156 weeks of crypto
sessions and benchmarked against 281 weeks of London session data.
KEY PERFORMANCE METRICS (BTC)
132% Invalidation Rate: Hit in 78.7% of sessions (Primary Stop Logic)
-100% Target Hit Rate: 82.2%
-168% Target Hit Rate: 73.3%
Regime Reliability: Fast Regime (89.3%), Normal Regime (89.0%)
Critical Distinction: Range A refers specifically to the Friday Asian H1 session. It is
NOT the Monday London 15M candle range used in legacy forex models.
Application of the correct time window is the single most important factor for
model accuracy.

6. 132% Capture System
When the 132% invalidation level is hit, the session is not necessarily over. The
"132% Capture System" defines three scenarios for managing failed setups.
SCENARIO A: IMMEDIATE REVERSAL (34% FREQUENCY)
Price hits 132% and immediately reverses within 2 H1 candles.
Action: Flip position direction.

Target: 100% Range A in opposite direction.
Success Rate: 82%
SCENARIO B: RETEST & CONTINUATION (28% FREQUENCY)
Price hits 132%, retraces to retest the level 2-4 candles later, then resumes
original bias.
Action: Re-enter original bias after retest confirmation.
Probability: 76% Continuation.
SCENARIO C: FULL INVALIDATION (38% FREQUENCY)
Price enters random walk behavior or chops around 132%.
Action: Full exit. Log failed session. Await next Range A.
ETH Note: This scenario happens 52% of the time in ETH (vs 38% BTC).

8. Risk Management Framework
Strict capital preservation rules to survive high-volatility crypto environments.
Position Sizing
Max Risk Per Setup: 3% Total Capital (Combined BTC + ETH).
ETH Sizing Adjustment: Reduce BTC allocation size by 35-40% for equivalent
ETH exposure to account for volatility.
Correlation Cap: If BTC and ETH positions are open simultaneously, cap
combined size at 2.5% to avoid double-correlation risk.
Drawdown Protocol
Stop Trading Rule: Stop all trading after 3 consecutive 132% invalidations.
Regime Mismatch: Never hold a Slow-regime position > 6 hours. Never hold a
Normal ETH position > 9 hours.
Systemic Cascade: If 132% is hit on BOTH BTC and ETH within the same 4H
window, flat all positions and skip the next 2 Range A cycles.
