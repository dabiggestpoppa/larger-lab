#   impulse_extreme = bar.high of impulse candle (the candle that broke out)

#   Then we wait for OCC: a candle that closes back in impulse direction (bullish)
#   Entry = OCC close
#   
#   The impulse candle high is the EXTREME of the breakout move
#   The OCC candle close (entry) is typically LOWER than the impulse high
#   (because there's a pullback between impulse and OCC)
#   
#   So: impulse_extreme (high) > OCC close (entry)
#   OLD SL = impulse_extreme = ABOVE entry
#   For LONG, SL should be BELOW entry
#   → OLD SL WAS ON THE WRONG SIDE FOR LONG ✓
#
# For SHORT:

#   The impulse candle closes BELOW the trigger level

#   Entry = OCC close
#
#   The impulse candle low is the EXTREME of the breakdown move
#   The OCC close (entry) is typically HIGHER than the impulse low
#   (because there's a pullback between impulse and OCC)
#
#   So: impulse_extreme (low) < OCC close (entry)
#   OLD SL = impulse_extreme = BELOW entry
#   For SHORT, SL should be ABOVE entry
#   → OLD SL WAS ON THE WRONG SIDE FOR SHORT ✓
