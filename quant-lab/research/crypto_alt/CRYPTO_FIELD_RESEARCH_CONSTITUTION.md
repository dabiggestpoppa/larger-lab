# CRYPTO FIELD RESEARCH CONSTITUTION

**Branch:** `agent/crypto-quant-foundry`  
**Status:** Governing research doctrine  
**Scope:** Top-500 crypto terrain / mechanism research  

## Core Objective

Find the smallest non-redundant set of observable primitives sufficient to reconstruct the major recurring state transitions of the crypto ecosystem.

The goal is not to maximize indicators, model complexity, or trading rules. The goal is to understand the field well enough that any eventual execution logic can remain simple.

## Governing Research Order

**Observe -> Compress -> Perturb -> Reconstruct -> Localize -> Formalize.**

Do not begin with advanced mathematics because the mathematics is available. Start from the market question, determine what is actually observable, remove redundant descriptions, test what survives controlled removal, reconstruct the phenomenon from multiple views, locate where structure changes, and only then select the mathematics that best describes the surviving object.

## Epistemic Separation

Maintain three distinct layers:

1. **Real market state** — the full economic system, including variables that may be permanently or temporarily hidden.
2. **Observable projection** — the data surfaces available to us: price, rank, market cap, volume, TVL, stablecoin supply, bridge flow, DEX activity, wallet labels, funding, OI, venue state, etc.
3. **Inferred structure** — the latent states, mechanisms, relationships, geometry, hierarchy, and causal hypotheses reconstructed from those observations.

Never confuse inferred structure with the full real state.

**Permanent law:** Never claim more resolution than the observation layer can support.

## Unknown-Information Question

For every research problem, explicitly ask:

- What information can we observe directly?
- What information is only indirectly observable?
- What information is missing because our sensors/data sources do not cover it?
- What information may be fundamentally unavailable to us?
- Could apparent randomness or an 80% pattern be the result of an unobserved conditioning variable rather than genuine stochastic failure?

Do not assume every incomplete pattern can become deterministic. Instead, treat residual failure as a candidate hidden-state problem until evidence supports irreducible uncertainty.

## Redundancy Doctrine

Many variables may be multiple sensors for the same latent phenomenon.

Example candidates:

- BTC return
- total-market return
- Top-10 breadth
- ETH return
- market beta
- risk-on measures

may all partially encode a common field pressure.

For every candidate variable X ask:

- If X is removed, does the phenomenon remain?
- If X is removed, does only one local region change?
- Does X add information after its correlated proxies are controlled?
- Can another variable substitute for X without materially changing reconstruction quality?

Classify observations as:

- **REDUNDANT_PROXY**
- **PARTIAL_PROXY**
- **LOCAL_COORDINATE**
- **DISTINCT_INFORMATION**
- **CANDIDATE_PRIMITIVE**

Dimensionality reduction is a consequence of redundancy discovery, not the objective by itself.

## Primitive Doctrine

A primitive is not simply a low-level raw variable.

A **candidate primitive** is a minimally reducible factor, constraint, or state variable whose removal materially alters one or more recurring phenomena and which cannot be replaced by an equivalent observable without loss of explanatory structure.

Potential primitives must be discovered empirically. Examples such as liquidity, capital concentration, deployable collateral, leverage, supply mobility, usage, attention, or structural connectivity are hypotheses only.

## Perturbation Doctrine

Every important pattern should face controlled removal and perturbation.

Examples:

- remove BTC/global beta
- remove one cycle
- remove one chain
- remove one rank band
- remove one sector
- remove a candidate flow factor
- condition away broad volatility
- truncate the data before a future period
- substitute equivalent proxies

Then measure:

- whether the pattern survives
- how much its geometry changes
- where the change occurs
- whether direction, timing, persistence, or topology changes

The objective is not merely robustness. Perturbation is a tool for discovering which coordinates govern which parts of the field.

## Multi-View Observation

The same phenomenon should be observed from multiple resolutions whenever data permits.

Example: ecosystem expansion can be viewed through:

- market level
- chain level
- sector/subsector level
- rank-band level
- individual asset level
- wallet/holder level
- venue/execution level

Agreement across independent views increases confidence in reconstruction. Disagreement is not noise to discard; it may identify hidden state, lagged propagation, local decoupling, or measurement failure.

## Reconstruction Doctrine

Treat terrain research as an inverse problem:

> Given multiple observable projections, what latent state or mechanism most parsimoniously reconstructs the phenomenon?

Reconstruction quality must be judged against simpler alternatives and must preserve uncertainty. A latent-state label is not truth merely because it fits the data.

## Plateau Doctrine

Distinguish two plateaus.

### Information Plateau

Additional observables stop materially improving reconstruction or state discrimination.

Question: **Have we learned enough from the available sensors?**

### Field / State Plateau

The market phenomenon itself stops propagating, migrating, expanding, or contracting.

Question: **What constraint caused the system to stall, and what event or state transition releases the stall?**

Do not confuse these two.

## Node / Merge / Dissolve Rule

Every research answer should update the field map through one of three operations:

### NEW NODE
A genuinely new primitive, state, relationship, boundary, or mechanism appears.

### MERGE
Two or more previously separate observations are shown to encode substantially the same latent information or transformation.

### DISSOLVE
A previously observed pattern disappears after the correct control, perturbation, PIT correction, or conditioning state is applied.

This is the governing compression loop.

## Scientific Escalation Rule

Use the simplest formalism that answers the question.

Escalate only when the surviving empirical object requires it:

- **Set theory** when overlapping membership, inclusion, intersection, or transition between sets is central.
- **Linear/vector geometry** when redundant or independent coordinates and projections are central.
- **Graph theory** when connectivity and propagation among identifiable nodes/edges is central.
- **Topology** when connectivity classes, holes, boundaries, persistence, bridges, or deformation-invariant structure matter.
- **Dynamical systems** when recurrent states, attractors, transition paths, hysteresis, bifurcation, or metastability appear.
- **Category theory** when empirically supported transformations compose across different objects/scales and the transformation structure matters more than the object names.
- **Recursive / abstract geometry** when materially similar structures recur across asset, sector, chain, rank, and market scales.
- **Information theory** when incremental information, redundancy, entropy, transfer, or hidden-state reconstruction is the concrete question.

Advanced mathematics must clarify an observed structure, not manufacture one.

## Causality Doctrine

Do not jump from correlation to cause.

For each major relationship ask:

1. Does A and B merely co-move?
2. Does A reliably precede B?
3. Does the ordering persist under relevant state conditions?
4. Does it survive common-factor removal?
5. Does it survive subperiod/cycle removal?
6. Is there a defensible transmission mechanism?
7. Is A necessary, sufficient, substitutable, or only conditionally relevant?

Preserve observational limits in every causal claim.

## Flexibility Doctrine

Research methods are strict; hypotheses are flexible.

Be rigid about:

- PIT truth
- no leakage
- causal ordering
- test-count accounting
- null preservation
- statistical correction
- provenance
- falsification

Be flexible about:

- hierarchy
- direction of flow
- number of states
- scale of relevant structure
- which mathematics ultimately applies
- whether a favored pattern survives

Follow the field, not the original story.

## Strategy Boundary

Terrain mapping is not strategy optimization.

Do not convert every supported mechanism into an entry signal.

A mechanism may be valuable because it identifies:

- market state
- local field structure
- a boundary
- an exhaustion condition
- a causal bottleneck
- an independent liquidity island
- an information limit

without ever becoming a trade trigger.

## Governing Statement

> Complexity belongs in understanding the terrain. Execution should only inherit complexity that the terrain proves necessary.
