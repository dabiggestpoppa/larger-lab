## 1. Question before mathematics

LF9 established that the strongest local-physics phenomena are real:
continuous relational-state persistence survives a fair recheck; absolute
physical shock precedes local reorganization; sigma is secondary within a
physical-amplitude class; volume is redundant after conditioning on absolute
move; matched local shock response is largely invariant to global field;
EARLY_CONTAGION and PERSISTENT_DECOUPLING survive purged/FDR; the
downside/upside contagion asymmetry is robust; the relational-state
predictive null is frozen.

The next question is therefore NOT "are these phenomena real?" It is:

> WHAT ARE THE INTERNAL DIMENSIONS / SPECIES / TEMPORAL GEOMETRY OF LOCAL
> PHYSICAL SHOCK, CONTAGION, DECOUPLING, AND DIRECTIONAL ASYMMETRY?

This is a cartography checkpoint. We start broad, measure a rich set of
continuous local coordinates, and only then compress into species. We do not
pre-name species; names must be earned from data (n >= 50, >= 3 subperiods,
heldout stable).

## 4. Statistically precommitted

- Support bar: subtype/species requires n >= 50, >= 3 subperiods, heldout
  stable (LF8/LF9 convention).
- Purged separation: 3-fold subperiod-purged logistic AUC where predictive.
- FDR: BH-FDR at q <= 0.10 across subtype / control-match scans.
- Matched controls: exact or caliper matching on confounders, then compare the
  residual differences (earliest-contagion vs non-contagious).
- Temporal map daily unless hourly data is available and PIT-safe (it is not in
  this substrate — we report daily and say so).
- Descriptive only; no mediation with causal labels, no manifold math unless a
  genuinely continuous structure appears (otherwise we phrase "continuous
  space").
