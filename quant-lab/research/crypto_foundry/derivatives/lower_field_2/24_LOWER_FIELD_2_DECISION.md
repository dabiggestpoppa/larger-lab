### Decision statement

LOWER-FIELD-2 confirms and hardens the lower-field structural terrain:

1. **The deep-rank downside-reversal effect is real**, monotonic with rank, and
   survives overlap purging (30D) on a ~7k effective-event sample. It is a
   genuine sign × rank × breadth interaction, not an outlier or overlap artifact.
2. **A reproducible cross-field state gate exists**: Top-500 breadth level +
   the DISP_HI|BRD_HI band-state raise lower-field tail delivery by ~5pp
   (realization) and ~14-18pp (sequence lift) — **retained as the primary
   handoff mechanism, replacing the failed MECH-4 EXIT-event hypothesis.**
3. **The single most important correction** is the **re-scoping of the LF1
   tail-activation claim**: the gradient is a field-wide depth/volatility
   effect, and SHMC is instead the lowest-tail (reverting) state. The genuinely
   high-tail state is SHORT_HOT_MEDIUM_HOT. This materially changes which
   distributional node is promoted.

### Limitations (why the gate is *with limitations*)

- Causal level is **L0/L1** throughout; no L2+ directional causation claimed.
  Breadth-vs-delivery is L1 (temporal ordering + conditional) and would need
  lagged/subbed stability work before any causal label.
- **ISOLATED-event cell N is small** at the deepest bands (36-612) — reversal
  and cluster anatomy at depth rest on thinner samples than their band-broad
  counterparts.
- **Time-to-peak is horizon-capped** (30D) — longer decay/reversal beyond 30D
  is not modeled (censoring not applied).
- **Liquidity Q5** and the pre-imposed vol-quintile lens are data-degenerate and
  excluded; the honest active-liquidity signal lives in Q4.
- The breadth lead-lag (velocity loads negatively) is an L1 signature visible
  in *this* checkpoint's daily OLS but was not preregistered as a multi-lag
  test; it must be tested with explicit lags in a downstream checkpoint before
  any temporal claim is promoted.
