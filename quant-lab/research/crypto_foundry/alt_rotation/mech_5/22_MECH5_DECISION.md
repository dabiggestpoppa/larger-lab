## Why PASS

MECH-5 established stable, perturbation-resistant additions:

1. **First-divergence structure (WS1, 16 variables).** Success and failure
   separate earliest and strongest on BTC 30D return (r=0.51, +0D) and breadth
   (r=0.53, +1D), with a clear temporal ordering: BTC/breadth first, ETH-rel
   (+2D), volatility/dispersion (+3-5D), chain TVL (+7D), dominance (+10D),
   rank-band velocity (+14-21D). L1 temporal-ordering evidence.

2. **Success/failure weight map (WS2, CV AUC 0.869).** Rank-band participation
   family is the largest incremental block (+0.028 AUC). Volatility adds nothing.
   State-age/timing actually overfits. Not a single primitive — a multivariate
   hierarchy led by breadth + rank participation.

3. **Two-clock mechanism (WS4).** Escape resolves fast (84% by 1D), propagation
   confirmation is slow (74% only by 7D). Escape-risk decays early; sustained
   success is slower to confirm. Distinct clocks reproduced.

4. **Conditional rescue (WS7, L2).** BREADTH_EXPANDING triples success rate
   (59.5% vs 21.6%, p_fdr<0.001); BREADTH_CONTRACTING / RISK_OFF / BTC_DOWN
   reduce it to ~5%. Breadth is the dominant gate coordinate.

5. **Recurring failure motifs (WS6).** EARLY_SNAPBACK (22%) and BREADTH_FADE
   (18%) are the dominant, reproducible failure geometries — two distinct ways
   rotation fails.
