# MECH-20 · 13 — GLOBAL / LOCAL CAPACITY ANALOGUE NOTE

**Question (MECH-20 §13):** is a common high-level capacity principle justified between the
global field layer (Agent 1) and the local neighborhood layer (Agent 2 / lower-field)?

## What the global layer shows (MECH-20 11–12)
- **GLOBAL CAPACITY is absorptive, not fuel.** Delivery rate falls monotonically with capacity
  in every load band (HIGH_LOAD Q1 0.81 → Q2 0.48 → Q4 0.45). High capacity + high forcing
  delivers less (0.36 vs 0.72 at low capacity). Saturation-without-delivery is more frequent at
  high capacity (0.15 vs 0.09).
- Capacity is a **state-structural attribute** (near-constant per 6-cell state), so its within-state
  role cannot be estimated — the between-state signal is that high-capacity states absorb pressure.
- Capacity **suppresses** threshold satisfaction and transfer efficiency (r −0.46 / −0.50) — it is
  not an enabler of the realization core (TRANSFER, AUC 0.83); it buffers.

## What the local layer shows (lower-field work, per Agent 2)
- LOCAL CAPACITY is the neighborhood's ability to absorb physical disturbance without structural
  reorganization (shock absorption / containment), with species-dependent absorption and damage
  accumulation that is mostly selection/composition (within-asset rho ~0).

## Verdict
**CAPACITY_AS_ABSORPTION_ROOM — SHARED_PRINCIPLE at the conceptual level.**
Both layers treat capacity as the amount of pressure a structure can absorb **before its response
law changes materially** — global: before activation→propagation delivery stalls; local: before
neighborhood structure reorganizes. The equations are NOT shared (global capacity is a state-level
attribute; local capacity is neighborhood-level, time-varying, species-dependent). No forced analogy,
no shared equations. This note only documents that the two ontologies are conceptually compatible.

- SHARED_PRINCIPLE: pressure-absorption-before-law-change.
- SEPARATE_OBJECTS: state-local vs neighborhood-local; different scales, different clocks.
- No merge proposed.
