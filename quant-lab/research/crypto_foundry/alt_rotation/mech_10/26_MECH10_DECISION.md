## What changed in the canonical field topology

1. **State age is now explainable.** The MECH-9 "UNRESOLVED" verdict is
   resolved: **BIRTH_AND_SELECTION**. HH persistence is mostly entry quality
   plus survival selection; within-episode maturation cannot be separately
   demonstrated (n=19, p=0.194). This de-risks any future use of "state age"
   as a standalone signal: it is a proxy for birth quality + selection, not
   a hidden within-episode process.

2. **Temporal delivery is cell- and age-specific.** Mature HH delivers
   (prop3 0.23 → 0.70) and stops snapping back (reentry 0.46 → 0.13);
   HL *decays* with age (prop7 0.214 → 0.031); LL stays inert. The two-clock
   structure (fast failure, slow propagation confirmation) survives within
   HH decomposition.

3. **PRICE_RECOVERY_RANK_DECAY is a distinct mechanism, not a label
   artifact.** The beta-rescue signature (broadest field, weak price, rank
   bleeding, 45% persistence, 30% slow rehab) is reproducible and has
   identifiable subtypes (WEAK_PRICE with 40% relapse vs STRONG_PRICE with
   rank repair +11). NEW_NODE / PRIORITY_EARNED — queued toward Agent-2
   health synthesis and future decay ecology.

4. **Stall-and-rot splits into FLAT vs DECLINING phenotypes** (breakdown
   46% vs 74%) — a refinement, not a merge.

5. **Perturbation-type effects are age-conditional.** MECH-9's "breadth jump
   lowers HH propagation" is localized to mid-age HH (4-14D); in mature HH
   dispersion jump is the strongest booster (+0.20). This sharpens the
   **permission (breadth) → realization (dispersion)** reading to a
   maturity-dependent form — descriptive, not causal.

6. **Route gates are mostly age-shifting; dispersion is the only stable
   gate.** Thresholds for breadth / rank_depth_rel / vol move with state
   age; dispersion holds position. Local geometry only — strong-form
   bifurcation stays NOT_EARNED.
