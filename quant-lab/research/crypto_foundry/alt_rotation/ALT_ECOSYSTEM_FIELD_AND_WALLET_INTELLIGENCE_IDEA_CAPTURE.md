## 1. Governing Thesis

The objective is **not** to build an altcoin signal factory.

The objective is to reconstruct crypto as a **living historical field**: what the ecosystem is made of, how its nodes and relationships arose, how they changed, what persisted, what disappeared, where liquidity lived, and how capital propagated through the network.

The edge is expected to emerge from **terrain, ecosystem structure, state transitions, and persistent relationships** before it emerges from any trading rule.

The guiding question is not:

> "Will BTC lead ETH and then alts?"

It is:

> **What is this ocean made of, what creates the tides, where do ripples propagate, what structures persist across cycles, and what structural forms recur even when the assets and narratives change?**

BTC → ETH → ALTS is one possible historical route, not a governing assumption.

## 3. Historical Ontology Must Be Point-in-Time

A project should not receive one permanent modern label and have it projected backward through history.

Where evidence supports it, preserve time-varying fields such as:

- `sector_at_t`
- `subsector_at_t`
- `economic_function_at_t`
- `chain_role_at_t`
- `ecosystem_status_at_t`
- `project_status_at_t`
- `dependency_graph_at_t`
- `token_role_at_t`
- `value_capture_at_t`

A project can change purpose, migrate chains, rebrand, merge, die, become dormant, or shift from one dominant economic function to another.

### Candidate project/ecosystem states

- ACTIVE
- GROWING
- DECLINING
- DORMANT
- GHOST
- ABANDONED
- MIGRATED
- MERGED
- REBRANDED
- DELISTED

**Death is a state.**

Ghost chains, abandoned projects, collapsed ecosystems, failed narratives, and obsolete market structures are part of the historical terrain and must not be removed by survivorship bias.

An asset should be able to belong to several overlapping categories simultaneously.

The ontology should therefore be a graph, not a single-folder taxonomy.

## 8. Morphisms / Structural Recurrence

The system should eventually search for **recurring forms rather than recurring token names**.

Possible example structure:

capital reservoir
→ infrastructure
→ primary application leader
→ adjacent applications
→ broad participation
→ speculative satellites
→ concentration
→ liquidity withdrawal

A 2021 DeFi cycle and a future cycle may contain entirely different tokens while preserving a similar state-transition geometry.

The research question is therefore:

> Do ecosystem state transitions repeat as structural morphisms even when the named assets, narratives, and technologies change?

Topology and dynamical-systems methods belong here **only after the underlying graph and historical state data are trustworthy**.

## 9. Three Separate Similarity Concepts

Future entity intelligence should keep these distinct:

### Semantic similarity
What projects say they are in docs / whitepapers.

### Economic similarity
What role they actually occupy: revenue mechanism, users, collateral role, value capture, product type, dependencies.

### Behavioral similarity
How they actually move, correlate, lead/lag, and respond to ecosystem states.

Differences between these similarity layers may themselves be informative.

## 11. Wallet / Whale Intelligence — New Required Terrain Layer

Large holders, treasuries, foundations, investors, staking entities, market makers, exchange wallets, and other concentrated holders can materially influence supply and liquidity.

Wallet tracking should become a dedicated future data lane rather than an ad-hoc signal.

### Important holder classes

- foundation / treasury
- team / insiders
- venture / private-round investors
- public-sale / launch participants
- airdrop recipients
- staking / validator wallets
- protocol-owned liquidity
- market makers
- centralized-exchange wallets
- bridges
- burn / null addresses
- smart-contract custody
- whale EOAs
- known funds / institutions
- unidentified large holders

Do **not** treat every top holder as a whale. Contracts, exchange omnibus wallets, bridges, LP contracts, burns and treasuries must be classified separately.

### Candidate whale-coverage concept

Rather than arbitrarily track every wallet, create concentration cohorts such as:

- top 1%
- top 5%
- top 10%
- top 30% of economically relevant circulating holdings
- holder-concentration percentiles

Exact thresholds must be empirically defined later and must exclude non-economic/system addresses where appropriate.

## 17. Guardrails

- Map before predicting.
- Mechanism before performance.
- Historical taxonomy must be point-in-time where possible.
- Do not force BTC/ETH as universal causal anchors.
- Do not force flow direction.
- Do not assume current ecosystems existed historically in the same form.
- Preserve failed, dead and ghost projects.
- Keep wallet labels probabilistic / provenance-backed.
- Do not confuse exchange/bridge/contracts with economic whales.
- Do not treat token unlocks as automatic bearish signals.
- Do not let semantic narratives override observed economic/market structure.
- Do not add topology/ML complexity until simpler graph structure earns it.
- Strategy remains downstream of terrain understanding.

---

## 18. Core Statement

> **Do not engineer complexity into the trade. Put the complexity into understanding the terrain.**

The eventual execution rule may be simple because the intelligence happens before the trigger.

The system's job is to know which ocean it is standing in, which current each asset belongs to, which currents persist, which disappeared, which holders control meaningful supply, how new supply enters the field, and which old structural forms are reappearing under new names.
