## Key results

- **State age (WS1/WS2)**: HH maturity is real (landmark fwd7 prop
  0.48 → 0.67, P(leave) 0.10 → 0.04) but partly selection — long-lived HH
  is born different (entry breadth/dispersion/BTC higher). Within-episode
  evolution (n=19) is directionally consistent but not separable. HH
  state-age maturity PROMOTED as NEW_NODE; HH birth quality DIRECTIONAL_ONLY.
- **Bifurcation (WS7/WS8)**: raw-coordinate binned surfaces show sharp
  regions (rank_depth_rel candidate, jump 0.31), but these are route-gate
  projections of already-earned gates. **SHARP_ROUTE_GATE_ONLY**;
  BIFURCATION_STRONG_FORM remains NOT_EARNED. State-space: LL attractor-like,
  HH active-but-stabilizing (|ΔX| 1.04 → 0.63 with age, p=1e-13), HL/LH
  transient corridors.
- **Health states (WS10/WS11)**: PRICE_RECOVERY_RANK_DECAY (n=282) is a real
  population embedded in the broadest field (breadth 0.388); price rebound
  durable (relapse 9.9%) but rank recovers only 31.6% by 30D. Rank health
  and price recovery remain separate clocks.
- **Stress response (WS12/WS13)**: RESPONDS vs NO_RESPONSE separate on field
  improvement and rank recovery, but first divergence is **+3D** —
  contemporaneous, not predictive. Surface verdict NO_STABLE_RESPONSE.
- **Perturbation (WS9)**: 8 local cells with |Δprop| ≥ 0.05; dispersion
  arrival into HH raises fwd7 prop, breadth jump into HH lowers it. Recovery
  latency median 1D. LOCAL_NODE.
- **Volatility (WS17)**: re-earned LOCAL_ROLE as intensity/retention inside
  HH (dwell 4.5D → 30D across vol terciles); NOT a route selector.
- **SHMC/SHHM (WS16)**: localized to opposite 2×2 corners (SHMC→LL,
  SHHM→HH); reversion vs continuation confirmed as LOCAL_ROLE only.
- **Liquidity (WS15)**: PARKED — no stable incremental role after controls.
