## 1. Window definition

- DURING: `2022-02-16 .. 2022-04-17` (61 days)
- PRE: calendar 2021 (`2021-01-01..2021-12-31`, 360 days)
- POST: `2022-05-01..2022-12-31` (245 days)
- Horizon context: 2022 = the full 2022 subperiod (338 days)

## 2. What changed (computed in `20_2022_SHIFT_RECONSTRUCTION.csv`,
`15_SATURATION_NODE_DRIFT.csv`)

- **Saturation response geometry collapsed** across every rank patch:
  - slope `k`: 2020-21 ~1.2–1.8, 2023 ~0.45–0.5, 2024 ~0.5–0.8, 2025-26
    ~0.35–0.5 → **2022 k ≈ 0.12–0.16** (roughly an order of magnitude flatter).
  - ceiling: hit the logistic clamp (~1.10) in 2022 vs ~0.51–0.93 in other years.
  - half-saturation: jumped to **positive forcing** (+0.90 to +1.94 in 2022)
    vs negative (−1.3 to −2.6) in every other subperiod.
  - Reading: during the window the field still attained high activation but its
    response became essentially **forcing-insensitive** (shallow, ~decoupled).
- **Exit / branch structure compressed:**
  - dominant exit share `p1` 0.36 (PRE) → 0.78 (DURING) → 0.62 (POST);
  - branch entropy 0.10 → 0.37 → 0.29; branch count k 0.53 → 1.39 → 1.09;
  - i.e. exits coalesced toward fewer, more dominant destinations.
- **Forcing level declined monotonically** common_forcing +1.12 (PRE) → −0.47
  (DURING) → −1.28 (POST), but `z_during_vs_2022 = +0.71` — the forcing level
  itself was **not** a universal outlier; it sat inside the 2022 range.
- **What did NOT change:** activation level (~0.46, ±0.03 across windows),
  breadth participation (~0.45–0.49), dispersion collapsed to ~0.15 but stable
  across during/post; topology self-transition ordering inconclusive in-window
  (few occupied 6-cells), but the 6/8-cell road system itself did not re-map
  (road freeze audit rho ≥ 0.67–0.94 full-sample).

## 3. Hypothesis adjudication (H1 vs H2)

- Activation/breadth speaks **H1 (known-variable, mild)** — levels were not
  extreme.
- Saturation geometry + exit concentration speak **H2 (distinct law / response
  shift)** — the *response shape and transition structure* changed, not just
  parameter extremes.
- **Verdict: H2_LEAN / MIXED — response-shape shift.** Not a single forcing
  extreme; a change in the response geometry (saturation shallow + exits
  concentrated). This is the same aligned window MECH-16 flagged, now with an
  independent mechanism signature.

## 4. Defining parameter region (descriptive envelope)

- k (saturation slope) ≈ 0.12–0.16 (baseline 0.4–1.7)
- half-sat ∈ [+0.9, +1.9] (baseline negative)

  one-subperiod**. No evidence it self-sustains into 2023.
