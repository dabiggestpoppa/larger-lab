## Headline results

**1. Full 4-state lifecycle (02).** 171 cell × age × horizon rows. Every cell
has its own probability-mass migration law. HH back-loads propagation
(fwd7 prop 0.44 → 0.90, reentry 0.70 → 0.24 from AGE_1 to AGE_15_PLUS) —
the MECH-11 competing-risk mass shift reproduces in a full lifecycle frame.
LL behaves differently: escape is fast (AGE_1 P(exit) 0.72) but settles into
dormancy (prop stays ~0.13-0.23 across all ages); LL does NOT mature toward
delivery the way HH does. HL/LH are short-lived corridors.

**2. Failure geometry (03).** All four cells show **DIFFERENT_AT_BIRTH** —
the success and failure mirrors of the same cell are already separable at
t0 (FDR-corrected, 2-6 significant coordinates per cell at lag 0). HH:
successors sit in broader/higher-dispersion fields (breadth 0.71 vs 0.59,
p=2e-19); LH separates on vol; LL on dispersion. This extends MECH-11's
failure-mirror finding: failure is a *different birth state*, not a late
fade, in every cell.

**3. Broad sequence search (03 → 04).** 13,067 sequence words over 17 atoms
and windows 1-14D: **0 COMMON, 0 LOCAL, 2 RARE**. The MECH-11 canonical
chain (BREADTH_EXPANDS → CONCENTRATION_RELEASES → DISPERSION_EXPANDS →
TAIL_UP → RANK_RECRUITS → PROPAGATION) does NOT emerge as a stable general
ordering when scanned broadly and FDR-corrected. The partial-order graph
(05) confirms: only **2 REQUIRED_ORDER edges** (STATE_EXIT → tail up/down,
which are definitionally trivial), 6 PREFERRED_ORDER, 83 EXCHANGEABLE.
The chain atoms are individually EXCHANGEABLE or NO_ORDER pairs.
**Interpretation:** the MECH-11 sequence was conditional on eventual
propagation; it is a *conditional delivery ordering*, not a general
field-grammar constraint. Do not carry it as a universal sequence.

**4. Constraint-resolution entropy (08).** **ENTROPY_COLLAPSE.** Next-cell
branch entropy falls with state age in HH (1.15 → 0.52 bits, dominant share
0.73 → 0.92) and LL (1.58 → 0.46). As a state matures the future branch set
narrows — consistent with the survival-selection interpretation of age
(MECH-10): old states are not just persistent, they are more *constrained*.

**5. Waterfall as threshold hierarchy (09).** **THRESHOLD_HIERARCHY_EARNED.**
Deeper rank bands activate (ppos≥0.55 episode onset) only under stronger
common-field intensity: dispersion rho 0.72 (p=0.008), vol rho 0.95
(p=3e-6), BTC7 rho 0.96 (p=5e-7), stablecoin 0.90. The "waterfall" is not
literal capital flow — it is a **differential activation threshold** under
common forcing (MECH-11 hypothesis confirmed).

**6. Rank-patch graph (10/11).** 5 patches internally coherent (r 0.97-0.98).
False-loner density declines with depth (UPPER_CORE 37.4% → TRANSITION
9.3%); tail share rises with depth (3.3% → 7.3%). Edges: all patch pairs
synchronize same-day (rho 0.89-0.96) with no stable 1D lead-lag — one
synchronized field, no cascade (reproduces MECH-11 WS9).

**7. Patch perturbation response (12).** 105 patch × perturbation ×
amplitude cells. Large vol shocks raise tail share everywhere (UPPER_CORE
0.03 → 0.12); breadth drops propagate down-return into all patches; deeper
patches show muted activation response to breadth-only jumps (consistent
with the threshold hierarchy).

**8. Peer-formation context (13).** **FIELD_CONTEXT_DISTINCT.** LOCAL_CONTAGION
events sit in markedly weaker fields (breadth 0.23 vs 0.39 for AMBIGUOUS,
p_HH 0.29 vs 0.41). Peer reorganization toward contagion is a weak-field
phenomenon; ASSET_REJOINS_PEERS / BOTH_NORMALIZE sit between.

**9. Metastability (14) + transfer flow (15).** HH is the only
**METASTABLE_LIKE** cell (long dwell, high stationary share 0.35, low flux);
HL and LH are **TRANSIT_CORRIDOR** (short dwell, high flux); LL is
ORDINARY_STATE (high share but short dwell episodes). Transfer-flow pilot:
dominant channels are HH→HH and LL→LL self-loops (flux 0.32 each) — the
field's probability mass is parked in the two diagonal cells.

**10. Loner placement (16).** **DISTINCT_FIELD_PLACEMENT.** FALSE_LONER
events sit in broader fields with more HH (p_HH 0.43 vs 0.35 for
TRUE_LONER) — false loners are field-carried moves; true loners occur more
in LL/dormant fields. Reproduces MECH-11 WS10 with LF6 consensus classes.

**11. Absolute vs sigma amplitude (17).** 4-cell 2D geometry within the
extreme-event panel. **HIGH_SIGMA + HIGH_ABS is the worst state**
(fwd7 −0.016, reversal 59%) and **LOW_SIGMA + HIGH_ABS the best**
(fwd7 +0.021). Absolute disturbance adds real information beyond statistical
extremeness (ΔAUC +0.012). Physical disturbance and statistical extremity
are NOT the same axis.

**12. Directional asymmetry (18).** **ASYMMETRIC_FIELD_GEOMETRY.** Family
breadth spread 0.425. MULTI_BAND_UP fires in the broadest fields (breadth
0.583, p_HH 0.59); ISOLATED_UPSIDE in the weakest (breadth 0.158, p_LL
0.53). Downside families are field-neutral (breadth ~0.31-0.33). Upside
events are field-selective; downside events are not.

**13. Potential→realization (19).** **LOCAL_CONVERSION_PATH.** The
participation→deconcentration→differentiation→tail→rank chain lifts
propagation monotonically (lift 1.02 → 1.41 from breadth-expansion to
rank-recruitment stage, n=318 at the last non-circular stage) but does NOT
reach the 1.5× threshold for a single CANDIDATE_CONVERSION_PRIMITIVE.
A real local conversion path exists; a single universal primitive does not.
