## Answers to the checkpoint's questions

1. **Does every state have its own mass-migration law?** YES. HH back-loads
   delivery (prop 0.44 → 0.90, reentry 0.70 → 0.24); LL escapes fast then
   parks (prop ~0.13-0.23 at every age, no maturation); HL/LH are corridors
   that decay with age (HL fwd7 prop 0.21 → 0.03 in MECH-10, reproduced
   structurally here). The 2×2 is NOT four copies of one law.

2. **Failure geometry per state.** DIFFERENT_AT_BIRTH in all four cells.
   Success and failure mirrors of the same cell separate at t0 (FDR-corrected).
   The mirror failure is a different birth state — consistent with MECH-11's
   birth-state divergence, now shown universally.

3. **Broad sequence search.** 0 COMMON / 0 LOCAL / 2 RARE across 13k words
   (17 atoms, 1-14D windows). The field does not speak in stable 3+ atom
   general sequences when scanned without conditioning on outcome.

4. **Partial-order constraint graph.** Only 2 REQUIRED_ORDER edges — both
   STATE_EXIT → tail activations, which are definitional (a state exit is
   required before a new tail regime). No required ordering among the
   "meaningful" atoms. **The MECH-11 5-atom chain is demoted to a
   conditional delivery ordering.** This is a genuine correction, not a
   failure: it tells us the sequence only organizes *given* eventual
   delivery.

5. **Constraint-resolution entropy.** ENTROPY_COLLAPSE — branch entropy
   falls with state age (HH 1.15 → 0.52 bits; LL 1.58 → 0.46). Mature
   states are more constrained, not just more persistent.

6. **Waterfall.** THRESHOLD_HIERARCHY_EARNED — deeper bands need stronger
   common-field intensity (dispersion rho 0.72, vol 0.95, BTC7 0.96).
   Waterfall = differential activation thresholds under common forcing.

7. **Rank-patch graph.** Coherent patches, false-loner density declining
   with depth (37% → 9%), tail share rising with depth (3.3% → 7.3%),
   synchronized same-day field with no lead-lag cascade.

8. **Patch perturbation response.** Large vol shocks raise tail share in all
   patches; deeper patches muted to breadth-only jumps.

9. **Peer-formation context.** LOCAL_CONTAGION is a weak-field phenomenon
   (breadth 0.23 vs 0.39 baseline) — peer reorganization toward contagion
   happens when the field cannot carry the shock.

10. **Metastability.** HH METASTABLE_LIKE; HL/LH TRANSIT_CORRIDOR; LL
    ORDINARY_STATE. Transfer flow parks probability mass in HH/LL
    self-loops. The light audit earns the metastable language for HH only —
    no spectral escalation.

11. **Loner placement.** FALSE_LONER = field-carried move (more HH, broader
    field); TRUE_LONER = dormancy/LL move. Distinct geometry confirmed with
    LF6 consensus classes.

12. **Absolute vs sigma.** Separate roles: HIGH_SIGMA+HIGH_ABS worst
    (fwd7 −0.016, reversal 59%), LOW_SIGMA+HIGH_ABS best (+0.021, ΔAUC
    +0.012). Statistical extremeness ≠ physical disturbance.

13. **Directional asymmetry.** Upside events are field-selective
    (MULTI_BAND_UP breadth 0.58 vs ISOLATED_UPSIDE 0.16); downside events
    are field-neutral. Up and down are not symmetric signs of one mechanism.

14. **Potential→realization.** LOCAL_CONVERSION_PATH — the chain lifts
    propagation 1.02 → 1.41× but not to a single-primitive threshold.
    A real conversion path exists; a universal primitive does not.
