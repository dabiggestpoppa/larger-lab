# Paper Summary: Meta-Learning the Optimal Mixture of Strategies for Online Portfolio Selection

**arXiv:** 2505.03659 | **Published:** May 2025 | **Authors:** Jiayu Shen, Jia Liu, Zhiping Chen

## Overview
Meta-learning framework that dynamically mixes multiple trading strategies (like a fund employing multiple managers) for online portfolio selection.

## Core Strategy Logic
- **Mixture of policies:** Simulate a fund with multiple "managers" (strategies), each suited for different market regimes
- **Meta-learning (MAML-style):**
  - Decompose long horizon into short tasks (time segments)
  - Meta-train offline on historical tasks to learn initialization that adapts quickly
  - Meta-test online: adapt to new regimes with few samples
- **Clustering:** Identify historically high-performing, low-similarity policies as candidates
- **Online adaptation:** Update mixture weights based on recent performance
- **Model-agnostic:** Can wrap any base strategy

## Reported Performance
- Excels in training time and data requirements
- Suitable for high-frequency algorithmic trading
- Demonstrates excellent **transferability and robustness** in cross-market/cross-period tests
- Outperforms static strategy allocation

## Features/Indicators Used
- Multiple base strategies (trend-following, mean-reversion, etc.)
- Clustering for strategy selection
- Meta-learned initialization parameters
- Online weight optimization

## Key Insight
The key innovation is treating strategy selection as a meta-learning problem. Instead of picking one strategy, you maintain a mixture and continuously adapt the weights. This directly addresses non-stationarity in financial markets.

## Implementation Notes for Quant Lab
- Highly applicable: we already have multiple strategies (stall_harvest, ema_cross, macd_momentum, etc.)
- Could implement a simplified version: maintain running Sharpe of each strategy, allocate capital proportional to recent performance
- The meta-learning component could be approximated with exponential decay weighting
