# Paper Summary: TradingAgents — Multi-Agent LLM Financial Trading Framework

**arXiv:** 2412.20138 | **Published:** Dec 2024 (v5 Mar 2025) | **Authors:** Xiao, Sun et al.

## Overview
Multi-agent stock trading framework mimicking a professional trading firm, built around LLM-powered agents with specialized roles.

## Core Strategy Logic
- **Agent roles (virtual trading firm):**
  - **Analysts:** Fundamentals, Sentiment, News, Technical — each analyzes their data slice
  - **Researchers:** Bull Researcher (builds long case) vs Bear Researcher (builds short case)
  - **Trader:** Synthesizes all analyses + debate → concrete trading decisions
  - **Risk Management:** Monitors exposure, can veto/adjust orders
- **Workflow:**
  1. Agents operate concurrently, each pulling their data
  2. Bull vs Bear researchers debate
  3. Trader synthesizes debate → proposes orders
  4. Risk team checks constraints → approves/modifies/rejects
- **Backtest:** Jan 1 – Mar 29, 2024 on large-cap tech stocks

## Reported Performance
- Higher **cumulative returns** vs buy-and-hold, rule-based, and standard DL agents
- Higher **Sharpe Ratio** (better risk-adjusted returns)
- Improved **Maximum Drawdown**
- Leverage kept within bounds (~2x max)

## Features/Indicators Used
- Multi-modal: prices, fundamentals, news, sentiment
- Natural language reasoning for interpretability
- Structured debate mechanism
- Risk constraints (position limits, VaR-like)

## Key Insight
The multi-agent debate mechanism (bull vs bear) forces the system to consider both sides, reducing overconfidence. The modular architecture makes it easy to debug and adapt.

## Implementation Notes for Quant Lab
- Adaptable to forex: replace equity analysts with FX-specific ones
- Technical analyst → price action, support/resistance, indicators
- News analyst → central bank decisions, economic data releases
- Sentiment analyst → COT positioning, risk sentiment (VIX, USD index)
- The debate framework could be simplified: score bull case vs bear case, trade the stronger side
