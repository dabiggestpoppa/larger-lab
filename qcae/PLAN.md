## System-wide invariants

- Standalone now. OCE-compatible by contract. OCE-governed later.
- Do not find repositories. Find reusable capability.
- LLM interpretation is analysis, never proof.
- Promotion requires evidence-gated lifecycle transitions.
- Repositories are containers; capabilities are the durable unit of memory.
- Prefer extraction/reimplementation when whole-repo adoption creates unnecessary coupling.
- Negative results must be stored as durable knowledge.
- Quant capability claims require independent validation before trading authority.
- Unknown code begins in a zero-trust sandbox.
- Every acquisition must be reversible.
- Net Capability Gain must exceed New System Burden.
