# QCAE Book III — Trust, Proof & Quant Validation

**Canon:** QCAE v0.1  
**Status:** IN BUILD

Book III is the proof firewall between an interesting acquisition path and anything Quant Lab may actually trust.

Book II answered what exists, where the capability lives, what can be extracted, and what must be tested. Book III answers whether that candidate is legally usable, safe enough to execute, reproducible, contract-correct, robust under adversarial conditions, performant enough for its intended role, and—when financial claims exist—valid under the CEREBUS research doctrine.

## Blocks

### Block 5 — Trust, Security & Legal
License intelligence, compatibility, supply-chain risk, secret/credential boundaries, isolation, data egress, and separation of trust scoring from authority.

### Block 6 — Proving Lab
Reproducible sandbox execution, upstream and independent tests, demonstrations, adversarial testing, performance benchmarking, and reproducibility packages.

### Block 7 — Quant & Financial Validation
Research-claim firewall, signal reconstruction, data integrity, independent backtesting, robustness, costs/execution, CEREBUS compatibility, and the separation of research capability from trading capability.

## Governing law

```text
SOURCE UNDERSTANDING ≠ TRUST
UPSTREAM TESTS ≠ CONTRACT PROOF
BACKTEST ≠ TRADING AUTHORITY
LLM JUDGMENT ≠ EVIDENCE
```

Unknown external code begins zero-trust. Promotion occurs only through explicit evidence-gated transitions.

## CEREBUS boundary

For trading/research capabilities, CEREBUS FX operational doctrine is authoritative. External alpha claims, strategy logic, statistics, and backtests do not override CEREBUS constraints. QCAE may acquire research capability without acquiring trading authority.

## History discipline

Every chapter and freeze receives its own commit. Book III changes the system's trust boundary, so later amendments must be explicit and include downstream-impact notes.
