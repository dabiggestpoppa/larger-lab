# QCAE Book III — Freeze Review

**Book:** Trust, Proof & Quant Validation  
**Status:** COMPLETE / FROZEN v0.1

## Architecture

```text
BOOK II FORENSIC PACKAGE
        ↓
BLOCK 5 — TRUST / SECURITY / LEGAL
Can we safely and legally attempt proof, and under what authority envelope?
        ↓
BLOCK 6 — PROVING LAB
Can the exact candidate build, execute, satisfy our contract, survive adversarial tests, and reproduce?
        ↓
BLOCK 7 — QUANT VALIDATION (when financial)
Is the claimed financial behavior causally valid, data-sound, robust, executable, and CEREBUS-compatible?
        ↓
BOOK IV — ACQUISITION / RECEIPTS / MEMORY
```

## Book III Core Transformation

Book III converts:

```text
"This candidate looks capable"
```

into:

```text
Here is the immutable candidate we tested,
what it was legally allowed to do,
how it was isolated,
what built,
which upstream claims reproduced,
which independent contract clauses passed,
how it failed under adversarial conditions,
what performance was independently measured,
and—if financial—what survived causal data, robustness, cost/execution, and CEREBUS validation.
```

## Cross-Block Checks

PASS — Block 5 authority constraints are consumed by Block 6 sandbox manifests.

PASS — Block 6 produces raw/replayable evidence rather than narrative-only conclusions.

PASS — financial candidates cannot bypass Block 7 merely because generic software tests pass.

PASS — license/security/functional/domain hard failures cannot be averaged away.

PASS — source understanding remains distinct from execution proof.

PASS — upstream tests remain distinct from Quant Lab contract tests.

PASS — performance evidence remains distinct from correctness.

PASS — CEREBUS compatibility remains distinct from profitability.

PASS — research capability remains distinct from trading authority.

PASS — standalone QCAE can enforce local policy while preserving future OCE authority migration.

## Book III Frozen Laws

1. Unknown external code begins zero-trust.
2. Legal compatibility is asset/acquisition-form/context scoped.
3. Source, artifact, dependency, data, and model provenance remain distinct.
4. Unknown candidates receive no production credentials or arbitrary network/data egress.
5. Sandbox privileges are derived from the proof task.
6. Trust assessment is never authority.
7. Build success is not behavioral proof.
8. Upstream test success is not independent contract proof.
9. Contract requirements map to independent evidence.
10. Proving seeks falsification, not only confirmation.
11. Performance benchmarks are predeclared, reproducible, and subordinate to correctness.
12. Raw evidence remains separate from evaluator interpretation.
13. Proof is revision/contract/environment scoped.
14. Financial claims begin untrusted regardless of source prestige.
15. Signal reconstruction must be information-time causal.
16. Data leakage/timestamp/session/cleaning assumptions are explicit.
17. Backtests are event-level auditable and experiment-identified.
18. Robustness includes regime/parameter/time/execution sensitivity and multiple-testing awareness.
19. Gross returns are not tradable net returns.
20. CEREBUS operational doctrine governs CEREBUS compatibility.
21. External models may support but cannot silently override CEREBUS structural constraints.
22. Failed alpha can coexist with useful reusable software/research atoms.
23. Research, decision support, and live trading capability are distinct states.
24. QCAE cannot self-grant production or capital authority.

## Book IV Entry Contract

Book IV receives evidence-qualified acquisition paths. It may choose wrapper/extraction/vendor/fork/reimplementation/integration forms, generate Capability Receipts, persist positive and negative knowledge, and monitor upstream drift. It may not reinterpret a failed Book III hard gate as a scoring inconvenience.
