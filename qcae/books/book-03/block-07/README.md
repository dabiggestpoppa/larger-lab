## Authority

CEREBUS FX Operational Manuals v3/v4 are the governing framework for CEREBUS trading logic, structural constraints, risk, and compatibility. External research can challenge or extend research questions, but it does not silently override CEREBUS doctrine.

## Critical distinction

QCAE may prove that code reproduces a published strategy without proving the strategy is valid. It may prove research value without granting live-trading capability.
