# Chapter 7.7 — CEREBUS Compatibility

## Mission

Determine whether an external financial capability can coexist with, support, extend, or contradict the CEREBUS constraint-resolution framework without silently changing its governing semantics.

## 7.7.1 Manual-First Rule

For CEREBUS trading logic, the operational manual is authoritative. QCAE must map external methods against manual constructs rather than reinterpret CEREBUS through external terminology.

The manual explicitly frames the system as probabilistic alignment with constraint resolution rather than prediction, uses structural boundaries/hard exits, and applies tier/regime-specific behavior. The v4 material also describes the Constraint Anchor as a structural activation at Asian constraint-band violation, with the opposite Asian extreme functioning as structural invalidation, while resolution amplifiers are profitable when aligned with the Anchor's direction. These are governing compatibility concepts, not optional stylistic preferences.

## 7.7.3 Compatibility Questions

Does the candidate:

- preserve constraint-resolution framing?
- respect structural invalidations?
- preserve tier/regime classification rather than flattening all conditions?
- preserve checkpoint/time semantics where the setup depends on them?
- align sizing/risk with governing constraints?
- introduce a predictive signal as optional evidence or as a replacement for the structural model?
- alter session/range definitions?
- require counter-direction behavior that conflicts with anchor alignment?

## 7.7.4 External Models

Machine learning, statistical signals, macro models, order-flow tools, or alternative execution engines may be acquired as supporting atoms if they improve classification, measurement, research, or execution without gaining authority to override structural constraints.

## 7.7.5 Conflict Handling

If external evidence challenges a CEREBUS rule, QCAE records a research contradiction and can propose a controlled research experiment. It cannot silently rewrite the manual or deploy the conflicting rule.

## 7.7.6 Tier/Regime Preservation

Validation must report performance conditional on the relevant tier/regime where CEREBUS defines such distinctions. Aggregate metrics that hide no-go/weak regimes are insufficient.

## 7.7.7 Structural Invalidation

A candidate that requires ignoring a CEREBUS hard structural boundary to achieve its reported performance is incompatible with that trading contract unless the governing manual is explicitly amended outside QCAE's inference authority.

## Invariants

1. CEREBUS manual semantics outrank external strategy logic for CEREBUS operation.
2. Constraint resolution is not silently replaced by prediction.
3. Tier/regime/structural boundaries remain explicit.
4. External models may support the system without governing it.
5. Contradictions create research tasks, not silent doctrine changes.
6. Aggregate performance cannot hide CEREBUS-defined conditional failures.
7. Compatibility is separate from profitability.

## Exit Criteria

QCAE can say whether a financial capability is native, supporting, orthogonal research, adapter-requiring, or conflicting with the CEREBUS framework and preserve that decision as evidence for acquisition.
