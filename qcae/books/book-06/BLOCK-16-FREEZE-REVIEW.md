## Frozen Qualification Laws

2. Core domain rules run hermetically without providers.
3. Every provider implementation passes shared contract suites.
4. LLM-backed workers are judged on contract compliance, grounding, uncertainty, and boundary behavior rather than prose identity.
5. Discovery qualification measures recall, precision, diversity, and cost.
6. DeepWiki must improve comprehension without becoming required for correctness or evidence authority.
7. Malicious repository fixtures cannot gain ambient host authority or poison canonical evidence.
8. License ambiguity fails closed rather than being guessed permissive.
9. Sandbox backend inability to enforce policy invalidates proof.
10. Evidence history/provenance must survive mutation attempts, migration, backup, restore, and federation.
11. End-to-end acquisition benchmarks span the entire acquisition spectrum and Capability Conservation traps.
12. Quant benchmarks deliberately include leakage, overfit, unrealistic costs, regime dependence, invalid CEREBUS semantics, and research-vs-trading authority separation.

14. Every confirmed production defect becomes a durable regression fixture.
