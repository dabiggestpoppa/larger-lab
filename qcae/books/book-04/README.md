# QCAE Book IV — Acquisition, Evidence & Intelligence Memory

**Canon:** QCAE v0.1  
**Status:** IN BUILD

Book IV converts proven capability into controlled Quant Lab ownership while preserving the evidence, reversibility, negative knowledge, and upstream awareness needed to keep that capability healthy over time.

## Blocks

### Block 8 — Acquisition & Integration
Choose acquisition form, build adapters, vendor/fork/reimplement safely, migrate incrementally, and preserve rollback.

### Block 9 — Evidence, Receipts & Memory
Create machine-readable Capability Receipts and durable positive/negative knowledge with lineage, confidence, expiry, and contradiction handling.

### Block 10 — Continuous Capability Intelligence
Monitor upstream and ecosystem changes, perform differential revalidation, detect capability drift, and refresh the acquisition landscape without repeating old work.

### Block 11 — Autonomous Engineering Intelligence
Turn QCAE inward: inspect Quant Lab's own architecture, detect redundant/custom capability burden, identify consolidation or replacement opportunities, and generate bounded engineering proposals.

## Governing transformation

```text
PROVEN CANDIDATE
→ ACQUISITION FORM
→ CONTROLLED INTEGRATION
→ CAPABILITY RECEIPT
→ DURABLE MEMORY
→ CONTINUOUS REVALIDATION
→ INTERNAL ENGINEERING INTELLIGENCE
```

## Constitutional boundary

Book IV may recommend and prepare changes. Integration/deployment authority remains policy-controlled. Standalone QCAE uses its local authority shim; future OCE governs these transitions without changing evidence semantics.
