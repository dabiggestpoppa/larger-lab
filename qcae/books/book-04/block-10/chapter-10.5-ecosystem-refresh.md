# Chapter 10.5 — Ecosystem Refresh

## Mission

Periodically ask whether better acquisition paths now exist for capabilities Quant Lab already owns or previously rejected.

## Refresh Inputs

Book II discovery sensors, package ecosystems, research/specifications, internal capability inventory, prior negative memory, and active receipts.

## Delta Discovery

Search for what is new since the last saturation point rather than restarting the entire discovery universe.

## Challenger Candidates

New candidates compete against the current integrated capability baseline, including switching cost. Novelty alone is not a reason to migrate.

## Rejected Candidate Revisit

Only revisit prior rejection when recorded reconsideration conditions are met.

## Invariants

1. Ecosystem intelligence is incremental.
2. Current internal capability is the incumbent baseline.
3. Switching cost counts.
4. Old rejections are not retried without changed conditions.
5. Popular new tools do not bypass Book II/III.

## Exit Criteria

QCAE can discover meaningful new alternatives without turning continuous intelligence into endless tool-chasing.
