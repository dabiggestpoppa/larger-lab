5. Last-known-good remains identifiable.

7. Quant drift is regime/domain evidence-driven rather than short-term P&L reaction.
8. Ecosystem refresh is incremental and incumbent-aware.
9. Negative memory prevents pointless candidate retries.
10. Continuous intelligence produces prioritized review work, not uncontrolled system mutation.
