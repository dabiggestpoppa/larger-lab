## Competitor Content Analysis

### Top Performing Content Types
1. **Transformations** — "I turned X into Y" format (highest engagement)
2. **Before/After** — Raw prompt → refined result (high saves/shares)
3. **Tutorials** — "How to create X with AI" (high saves, evergreen)
4. **Comparisons** — "Midjourney vs DALL-E vs Stable Diffusion" (high comments)
5. **Memes** — AI fails, funny prompts (high shares, viral potential)

### Common Hashtags
- #aiart #ai #artificialintelligence #midjourney #stablediffusion
- #digitalart #aiartist #aiartcommunity #dalle #flux
- #promptengineering #aiartwork #generativeart #neuralart

### Posting Patterns
- **TikTok:** 3-5x/day, peak hours 12pm-3pm, 7pm-10pm EST
- **Instagram:** 1-2x/day + stories, peak hours 11am-1pm, 7pm-9pm EST
- **X/Twitter:** 5-10x/day, peak hours 8am-10am, 12pm-1pm EST
- **Reddit:** 3-5x/day across subreddits, peak hours 9am-11am EST

## Content Gaps & Opportunities

1. **No dominant "AI Art News" account** — Opportunity for daily AI news/trends
2. **Few accounts do proper tutorials** — Most just showcase, don't educate
3. **No "AI Art Business" content** — How to make money with AI art is underserved
4. **Reddit is underutilized** — Most competitors ignore Reddit
5. **No multi-platform brand** — Most accounts are single-platform
6. **Prompt packs are underpriced** — Opportunity for premium prompt products
7. **No AI art "hot takes"** — Opinion content drives engagement
