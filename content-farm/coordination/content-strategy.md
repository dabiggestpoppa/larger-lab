## Platform Strategy

### TikTok (Primary)
- **Format:** 15-60s videos, image sequences with trending sounds
- **Frequency:** 3-5 posts/day
- **Content:** Transformations, tutorials, showcases, memes
- **Monetization:** Creator Fund, affiliate links in bio, digital products

### Instagram (Secondary)
- **Format:** Reels (1080x1920), Carousels (1080x1350), Posts (1080x1080)
- **Frequency:** 2-3 posts/day + 3-5 stories
- **Content:** High-quality showcases, carousels, behind-the-scenes
- **Monetization:** Affiliate links, sponsored posts, digital products

### X/Twitter (Growth)
- **Format:** Image posts (1200x675), threads
- **Frequency:** 5-10 tweets/day
- **Content:** AI news, tips, hot takes, threads
- **Monetization:** Affiliate links, newsletter funnel

### Reddit (Community)
- **Format:** Text + image posts
- **Frequency:** 3-5 posts/day across subreddits
- **Content:** Tutorials, showcases, discussions
- **Monetization:** Soft affiliate, lead generation
