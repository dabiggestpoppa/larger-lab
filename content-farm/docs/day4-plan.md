## 2. Engagement Strategy

### Comment Response Protocol
**Goal:** Respond to every comment within 2 hours during active hours (8AM-8PM EST)

**Response Templates:**

*Generic appreciation:*
"Thank you so much! 🙏 Which prompt are you going to try first?"

*Question about prompts:*
"Great question! The key is in the style keywords. I break it all down in the free prompt pack — link in bio! 🎨"

*Question about tools:*
"I use Midjourney for portraits and Stable Diffusion for landscapes. Both are amazing — it depends on your style! What are you currently using?"

*Negative/critical:*
"Thanks for the feedback! I'm always looking to improve. What would you like to see more of?"

### DM Response Protocol
**Goal:** Respond to all DMs within 4 hours

**Common DM Types:**
1. "Where can I get the prompts?" → Link to free Gumroad product
2. "Can I use these commercially?" → Yes, for your own content
3. "Do you do commissions?" → Not currently, but check the prompt pack
4. "Can we collaborate?" → Forward to MAD for decision

### Community Building
- **Daily:** Like and comment on 20 accounts in your niche
- **Weekly:** Share 5 community creations (with permission)
- **Weekly:** Host a "prompt challenge" in stories
- **Monthly:** Feature a "creator of the month"

## 3. Analytics Tracking

### Key Metrics (Track Daily)

| Metric | Target | How to Track |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Impressions | +10%/week | Platform analytics |
| Engagement Rate | 5-8% | (Likes+Comments+Shares)/Impressions |
| Follower Growth | +50/week | Platform analytics |
| Link Clicks | 50/week | UTM tracking |
| Email Signups | 20/week | Email platform |
| Gumroad Sales | 5/week | Gumdash dashboard |
| Revenue | $100/week | Gumroad + affiliate |

### Weekly Review (Every Sunday)
1. Review all metrics from the week
2. Identify top 3 performing posts
3. Identify bottom 3 performing posts
4. Analyze WHY top posts performed well
5. Adjust next week's content based on findings
6. Update affiliate tracker with revenue data

### Monthly Review (Last day of month)
1. Full metric summary
2. Revenue breakdown by stream
3. Audience growth analysis
4. Content performance trends
5. Adjust strategy for next month
6. Set new targets
