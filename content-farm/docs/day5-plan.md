## 1. A/B Testing Framework

### What to Test
| Element | Variant A | Variant B | Metric |
|---------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Caption length | Short (50 words) | Long (150 words) | Engagement rate |
| CTA type | "Save this" | "Link in bio" | Saves vs clicks |
| Post time | 9:00 AM | 6:00 PM | Reach |
| Hashtag count | 8 | 15 | Reach |
| Hook style | Question | Statement | 3-sec watch rate |
| Carousel slides | 5 | 10 | Completion rate |
| Reel length | 15s | 45s | Watch time |

### Testing Protocol
1. Test ONE variable at a time
2. Run each test for 7 days minimum
3. Document results in analytics dashboard
4. Implement winning variant
5. Move to next variable

## 2. Content Repurposing System

### The Content Multiplication Matrix

**One core piece → 10+ platform-optimized assets:**

```
Core Content (1 long-form piece)
├── Instagram Carousel (7-10 slides)
├── Instagram Reel (30-60s clip)
├── Instagram Story (3-5 story frames)
├── TikTok Video (15-60s vertical)
├── X/Twitter Thread (5-8 tweets)
├── X/Twitter Single Tweet (key insight)
├── Reddit Post (value-first text post)
├── Pinterest Pin (vertical, keyword-rich)
├── YouTube Short (vertical clip)
├── YouTube Long-form (5-15 min tutorial)
├── Email Newsletter (summary + CTA)
└── Blog Post (SEO-optimized long-form)
```

### Repurposing Workflow
1. **Create once:** Start with highest-effort format (YouTube long-form or carousel)
2. **Extract key insights:** Pull quotes, stats, tips for tweets
3. **Cut clips:** Extract 30-60s segments for Reels/TikTok/Shorts
4. **Reformat visuals:** Resize for each platform's dimensions
5. **Write platform-specific captions:** Adapt tone and CTA per platform
6. **Schedule:** Use scheduler to deploy across all platforms

### Repurposing Schedule
| Day | Activity | Output |
|-----|----------|--------|
| Monday | Create core content | 1 long-form piece |
| Tuesday | Extract + adapt | 5 platform variants |
| Wednesday | Create visuals | All formats designed |
| Thursday | Write captions | All captions finalized |
| Friday | Schedule everything | Week's content queued |

## 4. Growth Acceleration Strategies

### Strategy 1: Strategic Collaborations
**Target:** 2 collaborations/week starting Week 3
**Types:**
- **Shoutout swaps** with similar-sized accounts
- **Joint Lives/Reels** with complementary creators
- **Guest carousels** (create content for each other's accounts)
- **Prompt challenges** (co-hosted with another creator)

**Collaboration Outreach Template:**
```
Hey [Name]! Love your content on [specific post]. I'm building a community
around AI art and think our audiences would overlap. Would you be open to
a collab? I'm thinking [specific idea]. Let me know! — CerebusFX
```

### Strategy 2: Trending Content System
**Daily routine (15 min):**
1. Check TikTok trending sounds (Creative Center)
2. Check Instagram trending audio (Reels tab)
3. Check X trending topics (Explore)
4. Check Reddit trending (r/all, r/aiArt)
5. Adapt ONE trending format to AI art niche

**Trending Content Calendar:**
| Day | Trend Source | Adaptation |
|-----|-------------|------------|
| Monday | TikTok trending sound | AI art slideshow with trending audio |
| Tuesday | X trending topic | AI art hot take on trending subject |
| Wednesday | Reddit trending | AI art response to viral post |
| Thursday | Instagram trending format | AI art version of trending Reel format |
| Friday | Cross-platform trend | AI art compilation of week's trends |

### Strategy 3: Cross-Platform Funnel
```
TikTok (discovery) → Instagram (community) → Email (nurture) → Gumroad (purchase)
     ↓                      ↓                      ↓                    ↓
Viral short-form      Deeper content          Exclusive offers      Products
Mass reach            Engaged audience        High intent           Revenue
```

**Funnel tactics:**
- TikTok bio → Linktree → Instagram + Email signup
- Instagram Stories → "Link in bio" → Gumroad free product
- Email sequence → Gumroad premium product
- Reddit → Value-first content → Soft CTA to Instagram

### Strategy 4: Viral Content Formats
**Proven formats adapted for AI art:**

1. **"Rate This" posts** — Show AI art, ask for 1-10 rating
2. **"Before/After"** — Raw prompt → polished result
3. **"I Tried [X] for [Y] days"** — Challenge format
4. **"The [X] nobody talks about"** — Insight/hook format
5. **"POV: [relatable situation]"** — POV format
6. **"Things I wish I knew before [X]"** — Listicle format
7. **"[X] vs [Y] — the difference is wild"** — Comparison format
