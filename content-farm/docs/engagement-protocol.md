## 💬 Comment Response Protocol

### Response Time SLA
- **Priority 1 (Questions):** Within 1 hour
- **Priority 2 (Appreciation):** Within 2 hours
- **Priority 3 (General):** Within 4 hours
- **Priority 4 (Negative):** Within 1 hour (address quickly, don't ignore)

### Response Templates — Appreciation Comments

| Comment Type | Response Template |
|-------------|-------------------|
| "This is amazing!" | "Thank you so much! 🙏 Which detail is your favorite? I'd love to know!" |
| "Wow!" | "Thanks! 🔥 Want to see how I made it? Full prompt breakdown coming tomorrow!" |
| "Incredible!" | "Appreciate it! 🎨 Have you tried creating AI art yourself? I can share some starter prompts!" |
| "So beautiful!" | "Thank you! ✨ What style do you like most? I'll create more of what you love!" |
| "Goals!" | "Haha thank you! 😄 Save this for inspiration — more coming this week!" |
| "Fire emoji 🔥" | "🔥🔥🔥 Glad you like it! Follow for daily AI art drops!" |

### Response Templates — Question Comments

| Question Type | Response Template |
|--------------|-------------------|
| "What tool did you use?" | "Great question! I used [Midjourney/Flux/SD] for this one. Each tool has its strengths — I'm doing a comparison video soon, turn on notifications! 🔔" |
| "What was the prompt?" | "I'll share the full prompt in my next post! 📝 For now, follow so you don't miss it — I drop prompt breakdowns every week!" |
| "How long did this take?" | "About [X] minutes for this one! The key is in the prompt structure. I'm working on a free guide — link in bio! 🎨" |
| "Can I use this?" | "Glad you like it! For personal use, absolutely. For commercial use, check the license details — I sell prompt packs that include commercial rights. Link in bio! 💼" |
| "How do I start with AI art?" | "Welcome to the rabbit hole! 🐰 Start with Midjourney (easiest) or Stable Diffusion (most powerful). I have a free beginner's guide — link in bio! 📖" |
| "What settings did you use?" | "I keep a settings cheat sheet that I'm sharing this week! Follow + turn on notifications so you don't miss it 🔔" |

### Response Template — Negative/Critical Comments

| Criticism Type | Response Template |
|---------------|-------------------|
| "This isn't real art" | "I respect that perspective! Art means different things to different people. For me, the creativity is in the prompt design and curation. What kind of art do you create? 🎨" |
| "AI art is theft" | "That's a valid concern, and it's a conversation worth having. I believe in transparency — all my content is labeled as AI-generated. I also support human artists. What's your take? 🤔" |
| "Overrated" | "Tastes differ! I appreciate the honesty. What style of AI art DO you enjoy? Always looking for new inspiration ✨" |
| "Low effort" | "Fair point! The generation itself is quick, but the prompt engineering and curation take time. I'm working on a behind-the-scenes series to show the full process — stay tuned! 🎬" |
| Spam/troll | Delete and block. Do not engage. |

## 📩 DM Response Protocol

### Response Time SLA
- **All DMs:** Within 4 hours during active hours
- **Business inquiries:** Within 2 hours
- **Collaboration requests:** Within 2 hours (forward to MAD)

### DM Response Templates

**Type 1: "Where can I get the prompts?"**
> "Hey! Thanks for reaching out 🙏 I have a free 50-prompt pack that's perfect for getting started: [Gumroad link]. If you want the full 200-prompt premium pack, that's here: [Gumroad link]. Let me know if you have any questions!"

**Type 2: "Can I use these commercially?"**
> "Great question! The free prompts are for personal use. If you want commercial rights (selling products with the AI art), check out my Premium Prompt Pack — it includes a commercial license: [Gumroad link]"

**Type 3: "Do you do commissions?"**
> "I'm not taking commissions right now, but I have something better — my Premium Prompt Pack lets you create exactly what you need! It's here: [Gumroad link]. If you need something custom, I might have availability next month — I'll keep your info!"

**Type 4: "Can we collaborate?"**
> "Love the energy! 🔥 I'm always open to collaborations. Let me share your info with my manager and we'll get back to you within 24 hours. In the meantime, check out my media kit: [link]"

**Type 5: "How did you make this?"**
> "Thanks for asking! 🎨 I used [tool] with a carefully crafted prompt. I'm doing a full tutorial on this style — follow me so you don't miss it! In the meantime, my free prompt pack has similar styles: [link]"

**Type 6: Unsolicited promotion/spam**
> Don't respond. Block if repetitive.

## 🚫 Engagement Rules (NEVER Do)

1. **Never** use generic copy-paste responses (people can tell)
2. **Never** argue with trolls (delete and block)
3. **Never** spam other accounts with self-promotion
4. **Never** buy followers or engagement (kills algorithm)
5. **Never** use engagement pods (platforms detect and penalize)
6. **Never** post affiliate links in comments (looks spammy)
7. **Never** ignore negative comments (address or delete, don't leave)
8. **Never** engage with competitors' posts negatively

---

## ✅ Engagement Rules (ALWAYS Do)

1. **Always** respond to every genuine comment
2. **Always** ask follow-up questions to drive conversation
3. **Always** like the comment before responding (extra notification)
4. **Always** personalize responses (use their name/username)
5. **Always** direct people to your link in bio for products
6. **Always** engage authentically (read the comment, respond thoughtfully)
7. **Always** track your response times and improve
8. **Always** feature community content (builds loyalty)
