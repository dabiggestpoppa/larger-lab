## 🔔 Optimization Triggers

### Immediate Action Required (Within 24 Hours)
- **Engagement rate drops below 1.5%:** Pause posting, audit last 10 posts
- **Account flagged/warning:** Review content against platform guidelines
- **Viral post (>10x normal reach):** Engage heavily, post follow-up within 12 hours
- **Negative comment spike (>5 negative in 1 hour):** Address publicly, consider ethics post

### Weekly Action Required
- **Engagement rate declining 2+ weeks:** Test new content format
- **Follower growth <5%/week:** Increase posting frequency or add promotion
- **Link clicks <10/week:** Review CTA placement and link-in-bio
- **Email signups <10/week:** Improve lead magnet or add more CTAs

### Monthly Action Required
- **Revenue below 50% of target:** Review pricing, add revenue stream
- **Content output below target:** Fix pipeline or reduce frequency
- **One platform significantly underperforming:** Consider deprioritizing
- **New platform opportunity emerges:** Test with 20% of content budget
