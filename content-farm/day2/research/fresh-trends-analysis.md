## 4. Content Gap Analysis (What Competitors Are NOT Covering)

### 🔍 Identified Gaps

#### Gap 1: AI Art for Specific Professions
- **What's missing:** AI art content for doctors, lawyers, teachers, engineers, chefs
- **Why it matters:** Professionals want to use AI but don't see content for their field
- **Our angle:** "AI art for [profession]" series — "AI-generated medical illustrations," "AI for architects," "AI for chefs"
- **Estimated demand:** High (professional audiences have money to spend)

#### Gap 2: AI Art Ethics Deep Dives
- **What's missing:** Honest, nuanced discussions about AI art ethics
- **Why it matters:** This is the #1 debate in the space, but everyone avoids it
- **Our angle:** "The uncomfortable truth about AI art" series — balanced, not preachy
- **Estimated demand:** Very high (drives comments, shares, debate)

#### Gap 3: AI Art Business Blueprints
- **What's missing:** Step-by-step guides to making money with AI art
- **Why it matters:** Everyone wants to monetize, nobody shows the full path
- **Our angle:** "AI Art Business Blueprint" series — Etsy, Redbubble, Gumroad, client work
- **Estimated demand:** Extremely high (monetization content always performs)

#### Gap 4: AI Art for Kids/Families
- **What's missing:** Family-friendly AI art content
- **Why it matters:** Parents want to use AI with kids, but all content is adult-focused
- **Our angle:** "AI art with my kid" series — family-friendly prompts, kid-safe tools
- **Estimated demand:** High (underserved audience with spending power)

#### Gap 5: AI Art "Science Experiments"
- **What's missing:** Systematic testing of AI art variables
- **Why it matters:** People want to know what actually affects output quality
- **Our angle:** "AI Art Experiments" series — "What happens if I change CFG by 0.5?" / "Same prompt, 10 models"
- **Estimated demand:** High (educational + entertaining + shareable)

#### Gap 6: AI Art for Mental Health/Therapy
- **What's missing:** Using AI art for emotional expression, therapy, mindfulness
- **Why it matters:** Mental health content is huge, AI art + therapy is untapped
- **Our angle:** "I used AI art to process my emotions" — therapeutic prompt journeys
- **Estimated demand:** High (emotional content drives deep engagement)
