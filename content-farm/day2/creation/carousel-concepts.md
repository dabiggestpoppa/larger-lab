## Carousel 2: "AI Art Ethics — The Conversation Nobody's Having"

### Concept Overview

- **Style:** Thoughtful, balanced, editorial
- **Color palette:** Split design — warm left side, cool right side (representing both sides)
- **Font:** Serif for quotes, sans-serif for facts

- Layout: Split image — classical painting (left) vs AI art (right)

- Subtext: "Here's the conversation nobody's having." (smaller)
- Visual: Seamless split between classical oil painting and AI-generated art
- Emotional trigger: Curiosity + mild controversy

**Slide 2 — "What Everyone Says"**

- Bottom text: "Both sides are wrong. Here's why."
- Visual: Debate-style layout, slightly provocative

**Slide 3 — "What's Actually True"**
- Layout: Checklist style with ✅ and ❌
- Header: "The Nuanced Truth"

**Slide 5 — "What We Should Do"**

**Slide 6 — "My Personal Take"**

## Carousel 3: "I Generated 100 Images — CFG 5 vs CFG 9"

### Concept Overview
- **Format:** 7-slide carousel
- **Style:** Educational, scientific, clean
- **Color palette:** Purple (CFG 5) vs Orange (CFG 9) — contrasting colors
- **Font:** Monospace for data, clean sans-serif for explanations
- **Goal:** Drive saves (educational content) and establish expertise

### Slide-by-Slide Breakdown

**Slide 1 — Hook**
- Layout: Side-by-side comparison image (CFG 5 on left, CFG 9 on right)
- Text: "I Generated 100 Images: CFG 5 vs CFG 9" (large, centered)
- Subtext: "The difference is WILDLY unexpected." (smaller)
- Visual: Same prompt, two dramatically different results side by side
- Swipe indicator: "Swipe to see the science →"

**Slide 2 — "What is CFG?"**
- Layout: Simple explanation with visual diagram
- Header: "What is CFG?"
- Body: "CFG (Classifier Free Guidance) controls how closely the AI follows your prompt."
- Visual: Scale diagram from "Creative" (low CFG) to "Literal" (high CFG)
- Analogy: "Think of it as a 'creativity dial.' Low = AI interprets. High = AI obeys."

**Slide 3 — "CFG 5 Results"**
- Layout: Grid of 4-6 images generated with CFG 5
- Header: "CFG 5 — The Artist"
- Body: "More creative, less literal. The AI 'interprets' your prompt. More artistic, sometimes unexpected."
- Key traits:
  - ✨ More artistic interpretation
  - ✨ Softer, more organic results
  - ✨ Sometimes surprising (good or bad)
  - ✨ Best for abstract, artistic work
- Visual: Purple-tinted border, grid of CFG 5 results

**Slide 4 — "CFG 9 Results"**
- Layout: Grid of 4-6 images generated with CFG 9
- Header: "CFG 9 — The Perfectionist"
- Body: "More literal, less creative. The AI follows your prompt exactly. More accurate, sometimes rigid."
- Key traits:
  - 🎯 Follows prompt precisely
  - 🎯 More detailed and sharp
  - 🎯 Can look "overcooked" at very high values
  - 🎯 Best for specific compositions
- Visual: Orange-tinted border, grid of CFG 9 results

**Slide 5 — "Side-by-Side Comparison"**
- Layout: 3 rows of side-by-side comparisons (same prompt, different CFG)
- Header: "Same Prompt, Different CFG"
- Row 1: Portrait (CFG 5 vs CFG 7 vs CFG 9)
- Row 2: Landscape (CFG 5 vs CFG 7 vs CFG 9)
- Row 3: Abstract (CFG 5 vs CFG 7 vs CFG 9)
- Key insight: "Notice how CFG 5 is more 'painterly' while CFG 9 is more 'photographic'"

**Slide 6 — "The Sweet Spot"**
- Layout: Recommendation-focused, clean design
- Header: "The Sweet Spot: CFG 7"
- Body: "For most work, CFG 7 gives the best balance of creativity and accuracy."
- Quick guide:
  - "CFG 5-6: Abstract, artistic, experimental"
  - "CFG 7: General purpose, best all-around"
  - "CFG 8-9: Specific compositions, technical work"
  - "CFG 10+: Experimental, often overcooked"
- Pro tip: "Start at CFG 7, then adjust based on results."

**Slide 7 — CTA**
- Layout: Educational CTA, save-focused
- Header: "Save this for your next generation 🔖"
- Body: "CFG is just one of many settings that affect your results. Follow for weekly AI experiments."
- Secondary: "💬 What CFG do you use most? Drop your go-to settings below 👇"
- Visual: Clean, educational, community-building

## Production Notes for All Carousels

### Design Tools:
- **Canva AI** — Fastest option, templates available
- **Adobe Firefly** — Commercial-safe, integrated with CC
- **Figma** — Most control, best for custom designs
- **Midjourney/Flux** — Generate background images and visual elements

### Image Generation:
- Use the CivitAI API with the prompts from the content briefs
- Generate 3-5 variations per slide, pick the best
- Upscale all images to 1080x1350 (Instagram carousel optimal size)

### Export Settings:
- Format: PNG for quality, JPEG for smaller file size
- Resolution: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 ratio, optimal for Instagram)
- Color space: sRGB
- File naming: carousel-[number]-[slide]-[version].png

### Posting Strategy:
- Carousel 1 (Business): Post Tuesday 11 AM EST (highest IG engagement)
- Carousel 2 (Ethics): Post Thursday 7 PM EST (discussion-friendly time)
- Carousel 3 (Experiment): Post Wednesday 12 PM EST (educational content peak)
