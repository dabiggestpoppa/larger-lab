# Day 2 Marketing Brief — Content Farm

> **Date:** 2026-05-18 | **Phase:** Day 2 / Phase 2
> **Owner:** Marketing Agent
> **Priority:** HIGH — monetization pipeline

---

## Objective

Build out the marketing funnel: Week 2 campaign, ad copy variations, email nurture, media kit, and affiliate program research. Prepare everything for launch once MAD provides platform access.

---

## Tasks (in order)

### 1. Week 2 Launch Campaign (Days 8-14)
- Build on Day 1's `launch-campaign.md`
- Day-by-day plan: what to post, when, on which platform
- Include: content references (link to Creation agent's briefs), engagement tactics, cross-promotion
- Save to: `agents/marketing-ads/campaigns/launch-campaign-week2.md`

### 2. 20 Ad Copy Variations (A/B Test Ready)
- 10 headlines (short, < 10 words, different angles: curiosity, urgency, value, fear-of-missing-out)
- 10 body copy variations (2-3 sentences each, different hooks)
- Target: Gumroad prompt packs (both beginner and advanced)
- Save to: `agents/marketing-ads/copy/ad-copy-variations.md`

### 3. Email Nurture Sequence (5-email welcome series)
- Coordinate with Creation agent's email templates
- Focus on the marketing angle: subject lines, preview text, CTAs
- Email 1: Deliver lead magnet + set expectations
- Email 2: Share best-performing content + build trust
- Email 3: Case study / transformation story
- Email 4: Product recommendation (soft sell)
- Email 5: Limited offer + urgency (hard sell)
- Save to: `agents/marketing-ads/campaigns/email-nurture-sequence.md`

### 4. Media Kit Template
- One-page media kit for future sponsor outreach
- Include: audience demographics, engagement rates, platform stats, pricing tiers, contact info
- Use placeholder data where real data isn't available yet
- Save to: `agents/marketing-ads/reports/media-kit-template.md`

### 5. Affiliate Program Research
- Research 5 relevant affiliate programs:
  - AI tool affiliates (Midjourney, Leonardo AI, etc.)
  - Course platforms (Udemy, Skillshare)
  - Creative tools (Canva, Adobe)
  - Gumroad/affiliate networks
- For each: commission rate, cookie duration, requirements, signup link
- Save to: `agents/marketing-ads/reports/affiliate-tracker.md`

### 6. Gumroad Product Descriptions
- Write product descriptions for 3 products:
  1. "50 Viral AI Prompts" (Day 1 product)
  2. "50 Advanced AI Art Prompts" (Day 2 product)
  3. "AI Art Masterclass" (future product — placeholder)
- Include: title, subtitle, description, pricing suggestion, bullet points, FAQ
- Save to: `agents/marketing-ads/copy/gumroad-product-descriptions.md`
