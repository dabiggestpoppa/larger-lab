# Day 2 Research Brief — Content Farm

> **Date:** 2026-05-18 | **Phase:** Day 2 / Phase 2
> **Owner:** Research Agent
> **Priority:** HIGH — feeds Creation and Marketing

---

## Objective

Deepen the research foundation by expanding competitor analysis, refreshing trend data, and identifying new content opportunities. Everything produced today feeds directly into Creation and Marketing agent outputs.

---

## Tasks (in order)

### 1. Competitor Deep-Dives (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
- Pick the **top 3 competitors** from `content-farm/agents/content-research/competitor-updates.md`
- For each competitor, analyze their **last 30 days of content**:
  - Posting frequency per platform
  - Content types (carousels, reels, tweets, threads)
  - Engagement patterns (what gets likes/shares vs what flops)
  - Hashtag strategies
  - Content themes and angles
- Save to: `content-farm/agents/content-research/competitor-deep-dive.md`

### 2. Fresh Trend Data
- Update `content-farm/agents/content-research/TRENDS.md` with:
  - Trending AI tools launched in May 2026
  - New viral content formats (last 7 days)
  - Trending sounds/hashtags on TikTok
  - Emerging topics in AI/art/tech space
- Use web search — don't rely on old data

### 3. Hashtag Expansion
- Expand `content-farm/agents/content-research/hashtag-research.md`:
  - Add **50 new niche hashtags per vertical** (AI art, tech, productivity, creative)
  - Organize by: high-volume (>1M), medium (100K-1M), niche (<100K)
  - Include engagement rate estimates where possible

### 4. Content Gap Analysis
- Based on competitor deep-dives, identify **5 content gaps**:
  - Topics competitors aren't covering
  - Formats they're not using
  - Angles they're missing
  - Audience pain points unaddressed
- Save to: `content-farm/agents/content-research/content-gaps.md`

### 5. AI Tool Review Research
- Research **5 trending AI tools** launched or updated in May 2026
- For each tool: name, what it does, pricing, unique angle, content opportunity
- These become "review" content pieces for Creation agent
- Save to: `content-farm/agents/content-research/ai-tool-reviews.md`
