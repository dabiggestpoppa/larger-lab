## Objective

Scale up content production: more content briefs, second monetization product, email templates, and Week 2 calendar. All outputs should be platform-ready.

## Tasks (in order)

### 1. Second Prompt Pack (MONETIZATION — HIGH PRIORITY)
- Create **"50 Advanced AI Art Prompts"** pack
- Target: intermediate/advanced users (differentiate from Day 1's beginner pack)
- Include: complex style transfers, multi-concept prompts, negative prompt mastery, LoRA/ControlNet prompts
- Format: same structure as Day 1 pack (`agents/content-creation/prompt-packs/50-viral-ai-prompts.md`)
- Save to: `agents/content-creation/prompt-packs/50-advanced-ai-prompts.md`
- This is a **Gumroad product** — make it polished

### 2. 15 New Content Briefs
- **5 TikTok briefs:** trending format, hook, caption, hashtags, best post time
- **5 Instagram briefs:** carousel concept OR reel script, caption, hashtags
- **3 X/Twitter briefs:** thread outline OR single tweet + engagement hook
- **2 Reddit briefs:** post title, body, subreddit targeting, engagement strategy
- Each brief should be specific enough that someone can create the content without guessing
- Save to: `agents/content-creation/output/content-briefs-day2.md`

### 3. 30 New Captions
- 10 TikTok captions (short, hook-driven, CTA)
- 10 Instagram captions (longer, story-driven, hashtag-rich)
- 6 X/Twitter captions (concise, punchy, thread-starters)
- 4 Reddit post bodies (detailed, value-first, non-promotional)
- Save to: `agents/content-creation/captions/caption-bank-day2.md`

### 4. 3 Instagram Carousel Concepts
- Format: "Top 10" or "5 Steps" or "Myth vs Fact"
- For each: title, slide-by-slide content outline, caption, CTA
- Topics should align with AI art / tech / productivity verticals
- Save to: `agents/content-creation/output/carousel-concepts.md`

### 5. Email Welcome Sequence (5 emails)
- Email 1: Welcome + lead magnet delivery
- Email 2: Story + value (why AI art matters)
- Email 3: Social proof + case study
- Email 4: Soft sell (prompt pack or course)
- Email 5: Hard sell + urgency
- Save to: `content-farm/templates/email-templates.md`

### 6. Week 2 Content Calendar (Days 8-14)
- Day-by-day posting schedule across all platforms
- Include: platform, content type, topic, caption reference, best time
- Build on Week 1 calendar from Day 1
- Save to: `content-farm/coordination/content-calendar-week2.md`
