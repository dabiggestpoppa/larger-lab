## Email Best Practices

1. **Send time:** Tuesday/Thursday 10 AM EST (highest open rates for creative content)
2. **Subject lines:** Keep under 50 characters, use emojis sparingly (1 max)
3. **CTA:** One primary CTA per email, bold and linked
4. **Personalization:** Use {{first_name}} token, reference their interests if known
5. **Frequency:** Welcome sequence = daily for 5 days, then weekly newsletter
6. **Unsubscribe:** Always include one — it improves deliverability
