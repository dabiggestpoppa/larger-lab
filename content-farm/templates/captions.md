## Hook Formulas

### Pattern 1: Curiosity Gap
- "I can't believe this was made with AI..."
- "This prompt creates the most insane results..."
- "Nobody is talking about this AI trick..."

### Pattern 2: Challenge/Comparison
- "Midjourney vs Flux: Which one wins?"
- "Can you tell which one is AI?"
- "I gave the same prompt to 5 AI tools. Here's what happened."

### Pattern 3: Tutorial/Value
- "Here's how to create [X] in under 60 seconds"
- "Save this prompt — you'll need it later"
- "3 AI art prompts that go viral every time"

### Pattern 4: Story/Narrative
- "I spent 100 hours perfecting this prompt..."
- "This AI art style is taking over the internet..."
- "From zero to 100K followers with AI art"

### Pattern 5: Controversy/Hot Take
- "AI art IS real art. Here's why."
- "Unpopular opinion: [X] is overrated"
- "The AI art community needs to hear this..."
