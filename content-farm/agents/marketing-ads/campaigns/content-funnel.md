## 1. Full Funnel Architecture

### The Core Funnel: Free → Engaged → Monetized

```
AWARENESS (Top of Funnel)
├── TikTok viral short-form (AI art reveals, transformations)
├── Instagram Reels (satisfying AI generations)
├── X/Twitter threads (AI art tips & prompts)
└── Reddit value posts (free prompts, tutorials)
        ↓
ENGAGEMENT (Mid Funnel)
├── Follow / Like / Save on social platforms
├── Comment engagement (polls, "which one?" posts)
├── Share / repost behavior
└── Click link in bio (Linktree → landing page)
        ↓
CONVERSION (Bottom Funnel)
├── Free prompt pack download (email capture)
├── Affiliate link clicks (AI tools, courses)
├── Paid product purchases (prompt packs, presets)
└── Subscription signup (Patreon, paid newsletter)
        ↓
RETENTION (Loyalty Loop)
├── Email newsletter (weekly AI art tips)
├── Exclusive content for subscribers
├── Community (Discord, private group)
└── Upsell to higher-tier products (courses, bundles)
```

## 2. Platform-Specific Funnel Paths

### 🎵 TikTok → Instagram → Email → Paid

**Stage 1 — TikTok (Discovery Engine)**
- Content: 15-60s AI art reveals, "before/after" transformations, trending sound overlays
- Goal: Maximize views, follows, and profile visits
- CTA: "Follow for more AI art magic ✨" + "Link in bio for free prompts"
- KPI: Views, follower growth rate, profile visits

**Stage 2 — Instagram (Trust Builder)**
- Content: Carousel posts (prompt breakdowns), Reels (repurposed TikToks), Stories (polls, Q&A)
- Goal: Deepen engagement, build aesthetic brand identity
- CTA: "Save this for later 🔖" + "Drop a 🔥 if you want the prompt"
- KPI: Saves, shares, DMs, link clicks

**Stage 3 — Email List (Owned Audience)**
- Lead magnet: Free "50 Viral AI Prompts" PDF
- Content: Weekly newsletter with exclusive prompts, tool reviews, affiliate recommendations
- Goal: Build direct relationship, drive affiliate conversions
- CTA: "Get the free pack → [link]"
- KPI: Open rate (target 35%+), click rate (target 5%+)

**Stage 4 — Paid Products (Revenue)**
- Entry: $7-15 prompt packs on Gumroad/Etsy
- Mid: $29-49 style packs and bundles
- High: $99-199 courses and masterclasses
- CTA: "Want the full 200-pack bundle? Link in bio"
- KPI: Conversion rate (target 2-5% of email list)

### 📸 Instagram → Pinterest → Etsy Shop

**Stage 1 — Instagram (Showcase)**
- Content: High-quality AI art posts, carousel tutorials, Reels
- Goal: Build aesthetic brand, drive saves and shares
- CTA: "Save this aesthetic 🔖" + "Full prompt in comments"
- KPI: Saves, shares, reach

**Stage 2 — Pinterest (Evergreen Discovery)**
- Content: Pin all AI art to themed boards (Fantasy, Cyberpunk, Anime, etc.)
- Goal: Long-tail discovery, drive traffic to shop
- CTA: "Download the full prompt pack → [link]"
- KPI: Pin clicks, outbound traffic

**Stage 3 — Etsy Shop (Passive Sales)**
- Products: Prompt packs, print-ready art, digital wallpapers
- Goal: Passive income from search-driven purchases
- CTA: Product listings with SEO-optimized titles
- KPI: Conversion rate (target 3-5%), revenue per listing

### 🐦 X/Twitter → Newsletter → Affiliate Revenue

**Stage 1 — X/Twitter (Authority Building)**
- Content: AI art threads, prompt breakdowns, tool comparisons, hot takes
- Goal: Build authority in AI art niche, drive profile visits
- CTA: "Follow for daily AI art prompts 🎨" + "Free prompt pack → [link]"
- KPI: Impressions, follows, link clicks

**Stage 2 — Newsletter (Deep Engagement)**
- Content: "The AI Art Insider" — weekly digest of trends, prompts, tool reviews
- Goal: Own the audience, build trust for affiliate recommendations
- CTA: "Subscribe for free weekly prompts → [link]"
- KPI: Subscriber growth, open rate, affiliate CTR

**Stage 3 — Affiliate Revenue (Monetization)**
- Promote: AI tools (Midjourney, Leonardo AI, etc.), courses, hardware
- Goal: Earn commissions from trusted recommendations
- CTA: "I use [tool] for all my art — get 20% off → [affiliate link]"
- KPI: CTR (target 3-5%), conversion rate, commission per sale

### 🤖 Reddit → Discord → Community Monetization

**Stage 1 — Reddit (Value-First)**
- Content: Free prompts, tutorials, honest tool reviews, AMAs
- Goal: Build reputation, drive profile clicks
- CTA: "I compiled all my best prompts into a free pack → [link]"
- KPI: Upvotes, awards, profile visits

**Stage 2 — Discord (Community)**
- Content: Exclusive prompts, live Q&A, feedback channels
- Goal: Build loyal community, identify superfans
- CTA: "Join the community for exclusive prompts → [link]"
- KPI: Member count, engagement rate, retention

**Stage 3 — Community Monetization**
- Products: Premium Discord tier, exclusive prompt packs, early access
- Goal: Convert community members to paying customers
- CTA: "Unlock 500+ exclusive prompts → [paid tier]"
- KPI: Conversion rate, MRR from subscriptions

## 4. SFW → NSFW Funnel Design (Compliance-Safe)

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** All public-facing content is SFW. NSFW content is only on platforms that explicitly allow it (e.g., certain subreddits, Fansly, OnlyFans). Never mix SFW and NSFW on the same platform account.

### The Separation Model

```
SFW PUBLIC ACCOUNTS (TikTok, IG, X, Reddit SFW)
├── Build massive audience with SFW AI art
├── Establish brand identity and trust
├── Drive traffic to owned channels (email, Discord)
└── CTA: "For exclusive content → [link to NSFW platform]"

NSFW PLATFORMS (Fansly / OnlyFans / Reddit NSFW)
├── Smaller, dedicated audience
├── Higher willingness to pay
├── Exclusive content not available elsewhere
└── Monetization: subscriptions, tips, custom requests
```

### Compliance Rules
1. **Never** post NSFW on TikTok, Instagram, or X — instant ban
2. **Never** link directly from SFW platforms to NSFW in captions (algorithm suppression)
3. **Use** a landing page or Linktree with age-gating as intermediary
4. **Keep** NSFW account names/brands distinct from SFW accounts
5. **Use** Reddit NSFW subreddits as a bridge (SFW post → comment with NSFW link)

### Funnel Flow
```
TikTok SFW viral post
  → Profile link (Linktree)
    → Landing page (age verification)
      → Fansly/OnlyFans subscription page
        → Free trial content → Paid subscription
```

## 5. Affiliate Link Placement Strategy

### Where to Place Affiliate Links

| Location | Platform | Expected CTR | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Link in bio | TikTok, IG | 1-3% of profile visitors | Use Linktree or custom landing page |
| Pinned comment | TikTok | 0.5-1% of viewers | "Tools I used → [link]" |
| Caption (end) | Instagram | 0.3-0.8% of viewers | "Full breakdown + links in bio" |
| Thread end | X/Twitter | 1-2% of readers | "Here's the exact tool → [affiliate link]" |
| Post body | Reddit | 2-5% of readers | Only in value-first posts, disclose affiliation |
| Email newsletter | Email | 3-8% of subscribers | Highest CTR — warm audience |
| Blog post | Website | 2-4% of visitors | SEO-driven, evergreen affiliate content |

### Affiliate Programs to Join (Priority Order)
1. **Midjourney** — Check for affiliate/referral program (est. 20-30% commission)
2. **Leonardo AI** — Affiliate program available (est. 20-30% recurring)
3. **CivitAI** — Check for creator/referral program
4. **Gumroad** — Promote related AI art products (30% commission)
5. **Skillshare/Udemy** — AI art courses (up to 40% per sale)
6. **Amazon Associates** — Drawing tablets, GPUs, art supplies (1-10%)
7. **Canva** — AI design tools (recurring commission)

### Disclosure Requirements
- Always disclose affiliate links per FTC guidelines
- Use "#ad" or "#affiliate" on Instagram
- Use "(affiliate link)" on X/Twitter and Reddit
- Include disclosure in email newsletters

## 6. Lead Magnet Strategy

### Free Offer → Email Capture → Nurture → Convert

**Lead Magnet Options (Ranked by Conversion Potential):**
1. 🏆 **"50 Viral AI Prompts" PDF** — Highest perceived value, easy to create
2. **"AI Art Style Guide"** — 20 styles with example prompts
3. **"Free AI Art Wallpaper Pack"** — 10 high-res wallpapers
4. **"Prompt Formula Cheat Sheet"** — One-page reference for viral prompts
5. **"AI Tool Comparison Guide"** — Honest reviews with affiliate links

**Landing Page Flow:**
```
Social post CTA → Linktree → Landing page (email capture)
  → Thank you page (deliver lead magnet + affiliate recommendation)
    → Welcome email sequence (5 emails over 7 days)
      → Product offer (prompt pack or course)
```

**Welcome Email Sequence:**
- Email 1 (Immediate): Deliver lead magnet + introduce yourself
- Email 2 (Day 2): Share your best AI art tip + affiliate tool recommendation
- Email 3 (Day 4): Behind-the-scenes of your creative process
- Email 4 (Day 6): Social proof (testimonials, results) + soft product pitch
- Email 5 (Day 7): Limited-time offer on prompt pack (10% off)
