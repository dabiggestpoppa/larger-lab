## 5. Break-Even Analysis Per Revenue Stream

### Affiliate Marketing
- **Setup Cost:** $0 (free to join programs)
- **Time Investment:** 2-3 hours setup, 30 min/week maintenance
- **Break-Even:** Immediate (no costs)
- **Time to First Dollar:** 1-2 weeks (once content is live with links)
- **Monthly Costs:** $0
- **Profit Margin:** ~100% (pure commission)

### Prompt Packs (Digital Products)
- **Setup Cost:** $0 (Gumroad is free, takes 10% fee)
- **Time Investment:** 4-6 hours creation, 1 hour/week promotion
- **Break-Even:** Immediate (no production costs)
- **Time to First Dollar:** 1-2 weeks
- **Monthly Costs:** $0 (Gumroad takes 10% per sale)
- **Profit Margin:** ~90% (only platform fees)

### Print-on-Demand
- **Setup Cost:** $0 (Redbubble/TeePublic are free)
- **Time Investment:** 4-6 hours setup, 30 min/week optimization
- **Break-Even:** Immediate (no inventory costs)
- **Time to First Dollar:** 2-4 weeks (SEO takes time)
- **Monthly Costs:** $0
- **Profit Margin:** 15-25% per sale (platform takes base cost)

### Ad Revenue (Creator Funds)
- **Setup Cost:** $0
- **Time Investment:** Ongoing content production
- **Break-Even:** Requires minimum followers (TikTok: 10K, IG: varies)
- **Time to First Dollar:** 2-3 months
- **Monthly Costs:** $0
- **Profit Margin:** ~100% (but requires scale)

### Subscriptions (Patreon/Fansly)
- **Setup Cost:** $0-15/month (Patreon free tier or paid)
- **Time Investment:** 2-3 hours setup, 2-4 hours/week exclusive content
- **Break-Even:** ~10-20 paying subscribers at $5/month
- **Time to First Dollar:** 1-2 months
- **Monthly Costs:** $0-15 + platform fees (5-12%)
- **Profit Margin:** ~85% after platform fees
