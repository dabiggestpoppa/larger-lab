## 4. Content Gaps & Opportunities (Competitors Are Missing)

### Gap 1: **AI Art Education for Non-Artists**
- Most AI art accounts show OUTPUTS but not PROCESS
- Nobody is consistently teaching "how to go from zero to AI artist" as a series
- **Opportunity:** "AI Art Academy" series — beginner-friendly prompt tutorials, tool comparisons, workflow breakdowns
- **Format:** 60-second tutorials with on-screen text + voiceover

### Gap 2: **AI Art + Real Business Use Cases**
- Almost all AI art content is entertainment-focused
- Nobody shows how AI art generates REAL revenue (print on demand, NFTs, stock images, client work)
- **Opportunity:** "I made $X selling AI art" series — real revenue proof, platform breakdowns (Etsy, Redbubble, Gumroad)
- **Format:** Screen recording + earnings screenshots + step-by-step

### Gap 3: **AI Art Ethics & Authenticity Content**
- As TikTok cracks down on AI content (340% increase in removals), there's ZERO content about:
  - How to use AI ethically in content creation
  - How to disclose AI use properly
  - How to avoid getting banned
- **Opportunity:** "AI Content Survival Guide" — platform policies, best practices, staying compliant
- **Format:** Talking head or text-on-screen explainer

### Gap 4: **Niche AI Art Communities**
- Most accounts target broad "AI art" audience
- Nobody owns specific niches like:
  - AI art for tabletop RPGs/D&D
  - AI art for book covers/self-publishing
  - AI art for small business branding
  - AI art for tattoo design
- **Opportunity:** Pick 2-3 underserved niches and dominate them

### Gap 5: **AI Art "Roast" / Critique Content**
- Nobody is doing "I rated 100 AI artworks" or "AI art: good vs bad"
- Critique content drives massive engagement (comments, duets, stitches)
- **Opportunity:** "AI Art Roast" series — rate community submissions, teach what makes good AI art
- **Format:** Screen recording + commentary + community submissions
