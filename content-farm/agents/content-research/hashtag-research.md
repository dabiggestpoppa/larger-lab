## Recommended Hashtag Strategy

### Per Post: 15-20 hashtags total
- **3-4** High-competition reach tags (#aiart, #fyp, #viral, #trending)
- **5-6** Medium-competition niche tags (#aiartcommunity, #midjourney, #promptengineering)
- **4-6** Low-competition specific tags (#promptbreakdown, #beforeandafterai, #aiartshowcase)
- **2-3** Platform/format tags (#reels, #arttok, #shorts)

### Rotation Strategy:
- Change 30-40% of hashtags between posts
- Track which sets get most impressions
- Avoid using the exact same set 3x in a row (spam signal)
- Match hashtags to content topic (don't use #promptbreakdown on a pure showcase post)
