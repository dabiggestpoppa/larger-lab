## Revenue Streams

### Stream 1: Affiliate Marketing (Month 1+)
**Priority:** 🔴 P0 | **Effort:** Low | **Revenue Potential:** $500-5K/month

| Program | Commission | Link |
|---------|-----------|------|
| Midjourney | $1-2/signup | Affiliate program |
| Leonardo.ai | 20-30% recurring | Affiliate program |
| CivitAI | Varies | Referral links |
| Amazon (GPUs, tablets) | 1-4% | Amazon Associates |
| Skillshare/Udemy (AI courses) | $10-15/sale | Affiliate programs |

**Strategy:** Link in bio, pinned posts, tutorial content with affiliate links, "my setup" content.

### Stream 2: Digital Products (Month 1+)
**Priority:** 🔴 P0 | **Effort:** Medium | **Revenue Potential:** $500-10K/month

| Product | Price | Platform |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Prompt Packs (50-100 prompts) | $9.99-29.99 | Gumroad, Etsy |
| AI Art Presets/LUTs | $14.99-49.99 | Gumroad |
| Style Guide eBooks | $19.99-49.99 | Gumroad, Amazon KDP |
| Video Courses | $49-199 | Udemy, Skillshare |

**Strategy:** Free content → lead magnet (free prompt pack) → email list → product sales.

### Stream 3: Sponsored Content (Month 2-3)
**Priority:** 🟠 P1 | **Effort:** Medium | **Revenue Potential:** $1K-20K/month

| Sponsor Type | Rate | Requirements |
|-------------|------|-------------|
| AI Tool Companies | $200-2K/post | 10K+ followers |
| GPU/Hardware | $500-5K/post | 50K+ followers |
| Course Platforms | $300-1K/post | 25K+ followers |

**Strategy:** Build media kit at 10K followers, pitch AI tool companies directly.

### Stream 4: Ad Revenue (Month 3+)
**Priority:** 🟡 P2 | **Effort:** Low | **Revenue Potential:** $200-5K/month

| Platform | Requirements | Est. Revenue |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| TikTok Creator Fund | 10K followers, 100K views/30 days | $20-100/month |
| Instagram Reels Bonus | Invite-only | $50-500/month |
| X/Twitter Ad Revenue | 500 followers, 5M impressions/3 months | $100-1K/month |
| YouTube Shorts | 1K subscribers, 10M views/90 days | $100-2K/month |

### Stream 5: Community/Newsletter (Month 3+)
**Priority:** 🟡 P2 | **Effort:** High | **Revenue Potential:** $500-5K/month

| Product | Price | Platform |
