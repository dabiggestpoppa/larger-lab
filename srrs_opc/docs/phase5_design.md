## Goal

Maintain coherent adaptive continuity across long operational horizons through reconstructable trajectory fields, not persistent state storage.

## Core Shift

```
OLD: State → Persistent Memory → Identity Continuity (collapses under entropy)
NEW: Trajectory Fragments → Overlap Reconstruction → Repair Mediation → Directional Identity
```

## New Continuity Law
> Identity exists only to the degree that trajectory reconstruction remains viable.

## Architecture

### Trajectory Reconstruction Fields
Each observer maintains partial trajectory fragments. Collars reconstruct continuity overlap. Global continuity emerges from distributed reconstruction fields.

**Key insight:** Continuity is NOT stored state. It's the ability to reconstruct coherent directional trajectories from sparse overlap evidence.

### Continuity Collars
Long-horizon continuity emerges at **overlap boundaries**, not isolated memory stores. Each collar maintains:
- Temporal overlap (continuity intersection)
- Drift gradients (divergence measurement)
- Repair queues (contradiction correction)
- Reconstruction confidence (viability estimation)
- Entropy score (synchronization burden)
- Attractor compatibility (directional stability)

### Drift-Tolerant Identity Models
Identity tolerates local drift, partial contradiction, sparse discontinuity — as long as reconstructable directional coherence remains. Probabilistic, directional, constraint-bound.

Mirrors: biological continuity, distributed systems, OPH observer continuation, event sourcing replay.

### Repair-Generated Persistence
Persistence emerges from continual distributed repair under overlap consistency. NOT passive archival storage. Active, distributed, continuous, repair-driven.

### Temporal Attractor Stabilization
Attractors constrain viable reconstruction paths. Soft constraints, not deterministic state locks. Maintain: directional continuity, reconstruction constraints, repair preference fields, entropy boundaries.

### Entropy-Aware Continuity Scaling
Continuity must scale without infinite persistence burden. Continuously optimize: overlap density, trajectory retention, reconstruction locality, attractor sparsity.

### Minimal Continuity Realization (MCR)
> min(C) such that Reconstructable Continuity(C) ≥ λ

Minimum continuity structure necessary for recoverable trajectory coherence.
