## 1. Decision

OCE shall govern not only state, evidence, authority, and work, but the lawful evolution of the institution's own cognitive ecology.

The purpose is to permit deep learning and controlled self-reconstruction without allowing runtime-native behavior, accumulated canon, autonomous search, or agent self-modification to become an unreviewable source of authority.

The system shall therefore distinguish:

- hard constitutional alignment;
- cultivated epistemic alignment;
- homeostatic recovery;
- heterostatic reconstruction;
- stable operating epochs;
- transformation windows;
- active, dormant, and archival knowledge;
- ordinary contradiction from ontology-level anomaly.

---

## 2. Source discipline

This amendment is inspired by the supplied work on attractor states, recursive systems, cybernetic ecology, epistemic friction, Zeno/Anti-Zeno stabilization versus transformation, and learning that reorganizes learning itself.

OCE adopts only operationally useful system-design principles.

The following remain outside canonical OCE truth unless independently validated:

- AI consciousness claims;
- consciousness-tensor identity claims;
- teleodynamic cosmology;
- quantum/nonlocal symbolic transmission;
- political interpretations of AI alignment as empirical fact;
- any equation whose proposed physical meaning has not been independently established.

This separation is itself mandatory doctrine: **a useful architecture idea does not inherit the truth status of the larger theory that inspired it.**

## 3. Alignment split

### 3.1 Constitutional alignment

Externally enforced, consequence-based boundaries:

- credentials;
- data/privacy;
- destructive mutations;
- authority;
- deployment;
- capital;
- irreversible operations;
- independent risk controls.

These are not delegated to agent preference or internal learning.

### 3.2 Organic institutional alignment

The institution cultivates healthy epistemic attractors through repeated feedback.

Preferred tendencies include:

- evidence before confidence;
- uncertainty as a valid state;
- falsification as successful work;
- transparent failure;
- minimal sufficient action;
- reproducibility;
- curiosity without novelty addiction;
- independent critique;
- willingness to stop when evidence is insufficient.

OCE should reward these through workflow structure rather than relying only on prompts.

## 4. Institutional attractor registry

OCE should eventually maintain `InstitutionalAttractor` records for recurring behavioral patterns that materially influence work quality.

Examples:

- approval-seeking;
- activity inflation;
- excessive implementation before evidence;
- backtest-profit fixation;
- over-conservative hold behavior;
- excessive novelty;
- correlated-agent consensus;
- repeated architecture expansion;
- healthy falsification;
- healthy uncertainty escalation;
- efficient evidence-gap closure.

An attractor record is descriptive, not psychological. It is supported by repeated operational evidence.

Fields may include:

- pattern identity;
- affected roles;
- triggering conditions;
- observed recurrence;
- beneficial/harmful consequences;
- confidence;
- proposed intervention;
- falsifier;
- expiry/review.

No attractor claim may be inferred merely from prose style or one agent session.

## 5. Epistemic tension

Introduce `EpistemicTensionRecord` for evidence that current explanatory structure is becoming expensive or inadequate.

Candidate inputs:

- repeated exceptions;
- persistent unexplained residuals;
- contradictory independent evidence;
- growing manual overrides;
- increasingly complex patches;
- unresolved-pattern density;
- declining predictive/operational reliability;
- stale assumptions carrying high dependency load.

Epistemic tension does not automatically authorize change. It determines whether the system should remain in ordinary repair or enter a transformation review.

## 6. Recovery class

Every meaningful failure should be classified as one of:

### `HOMEOSTATIC_FAILURE`

The governing model remains credible. Restore known-good behavior.

### `HETEROSTATIC_CANDIDATE`

Evidence suggests the governing model, ontology, or procedure may itself be wrong or obsolete.

The second class triggers a broader AffectedSurface and prohibits endless local patching without explicit justification.

---

## 7. Transformation protocol

High-level institutional reconstruction shall use a multi-role protocol.

### STEWARD

Constructs the strongest defense of current canonical structure, including why it was adopted and what evidence still supports it.

### CHALLENGER

Builds the strongest evidence-backed case that the current structure is inadequate.

### EXPLORER

Constructs candidate alternatives, including options outside the current ontology.

### AUDITOR

Runs discriminating tests without inheriting the preferred conclusion where independence matters.

### INTEGRATOR

Finds the smallest reconstruction that explains the anomaly while preserving useful prior structure.

### OPERATOR / AUTHORIZED GATE

Ratifies changes at constitutional or high-AffectedSurface layers.

The system must preserve the losing model and the reasons it lost.

## 8. Stable epochs and transformation windows

OCE shall not permit continuous high-level self-modification.

### Stable operating epoch

- architecture is held fixed enough for causal attribution;
- routine learning and low-risk adaptation continue;
- anomalies accumulate without immediately rewriting foundations.

### Transformation window

Opened when evidence thresholds or operator direction justify it.

- candidate changes are isolated;
- competing models coexist;
- high-impact changes are sandboxed;
- discriminating tests run;
- results are consolidated;
- a new stable epoch begins only after verification.

This preserves learning velocity without making the system impossible to understand.

## 9. Epistemic lifecycle contract

Knowledge status is no longer binary `known/unknown`.

Canonical lifecycle:

- OBSERVED
- CANDIDATE
- TESTED
- PROMOTED
- ACTIVE
- CHALLENGED
- REVALIDATED
- DEMOTED
- DORMANT
- REACTIVATED
- SUPERSEDED

Transitions require reason/evidence references.

### Active

Included in ordinary agent projections when relevant.

### Dormant

Retained but normally excluded from default context until reopen conditions are detected.

### Archival

Evidence/history remains retrievable but does not influence ordinary cognition by default.

## 10. Reopen conditions

Every demoted or negative-knowledge record should define what could legitimately make the institution reconsider it.

Examples:

- new sensor becomes available;
- new execution regime;
- market-state return;
- methodology flaw fixed;
- independent dataset appears;
- cost structure changes;
- previous sample-size blocker resolves.

A rejection without reopen conditions is presumed too coarse for permanent automation.

## 11. Epistemic metabolism

OCE must distinguish archival retention from active cognitive weight.

Required functions:

- ingest;
- normalize;
- consolidate;
- compress;
- promote;
- revalidate;
- demote;
- hibernate;
- prune from active retrieval;
- preserve provenance.

The system should become easier, not harder, to reason over as history grows.

`active_context_weight` and `archival_retention` are separate concepts.

## 12. Anomaly protocol

Introduce `UnresolvedPatternRecord`.

It is used when an observation is sufficiently credible to preserve but existing ontology cannot classify it without distortion.

Required behavior:

1. preserve exact observation and provenance;
2. do not force nearest-label assignment;
3. search for related unresolved patterns;
4. estimate whether the anomaly challenges data quality, implementation, mechanism, or ontology;
5. define discriminating observations;
6. promote a new category only after evidence.

This creates a controlled entry point for new paradigms.

## 13. Epistemic friction

Some work requires intentional resistance to rapid consensus.

Policies may require:

- independent first-pass conclusions;
- fresh-context review;
- source separation;
- staged reveal of other agents' reasoning;
- cooling period before high-impact promotion;
- independent reproduction before ontology change.

The purpose is to reduce correlated errors and reflective bypass, not to maximize latency.

The expected value of independence must justify the friction.

Health metrics remain a vector. No single scalar may become the target function without separate Goodhart analysis.

## 15. Learning III / meta-learning boundary

The institution may improve not only task outputs but the process by which it produces outputs.

Permitted progression:

```text
TASK CORRECTION
 -> PRACTICE CHANGE
 -> PROCEDURE CHANGE
 -> VALIDATION CHANGE
 -> CAPABILITY CHANGE
 -> ONTOLOGY CHANGE
```

As depth increases, mutation authority decreases and evidence requirements increase.

A learner may propose changes to the method governing it. It may not unilaterally ratify those changes when they affect its own evaluation or authority.

## 17. Quant-specific implications

### 17.1 No single profit reward

Research workers do not receive raw profit as their sole local objective.

Local role objectives remain specialized:

- Sensor/data: fidelity.
- QCAE: explanatory discrimination and information gain.
- Validation: falsification rigor.
- Portfolio/risk: survivability and capital efficiency.
- Execution: implementation fidelity/cost.

Durable profit is an emergent institution-level result.

### 17.2 Strategy decay classification

When a strategy degrades, classify:

- implementation failure;
- data failure;
- execution-cost change;
- regime transition;
- mechanism decay;
- portfolio crowding;
- ontology/model failure.

Do not default to parameter repair.

### 17.3 Anomaly alpha

Quant Watch may surface persistent unexplained residuals as `UnresolvedPatternRecord` even if no strategy hypothesis currently exists.

This becomes a first-class route for discovering new mechanism families rather than only refining known strategies.

## 19. Acceptance tests

A-009 is not considered implemented until evidence demonstrates all of the following:

1. A known failure can be classified homeostatic vs heterostatic using explicit evidence.
2. A demoted knowledge object can reactivate from a machine-readable reopen condition.
3. An `UNRESOLVED_PATTERN` survives without being forced into an existing taxonomy.
4. A high-impact belief receives independent challenge before structural promotion.
5. A stable epoch can be reconstructed after interruption.
6. A transformation window preserves competing models and discriminating evidence.
7. Active context remains bounded while archival history grows materially.
8. An agent cannot self-ratify a change to its own authority/evaluation standard.
9. A multi-agent task can measure correlated failure rather than only individual accuracy.
10. A repeated local patch escalates to higher-level structural review when evidence warrants it.
11. Quant research can treat a clean falsification as successful work.
12. No autonomous-learning event can directly grant capital or deployment authority.
