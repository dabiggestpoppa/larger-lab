## 0. Governing decision

All planning in this index assumes:

> **One OCE, two development hemispheres, many domain institutions, replaceable cognitive organs.**

The operational implementation line remains `oce-program-build`.

This institutional line develops and stress-tests epistemic architecture plus future domain-institution plans. It does not duplicate operational OCE authority, persistence, workflow, artifact, evidence, or recovery infrastructure.

After G10 and operator ratification, surviving institutional deltas are to be **forward-ported** onto the then-current operational line through a dedicated convergence branch. No raw merge is presumed.

---

## 1. Planning law for every new program

Every new book must explicitly answer:

1. What OCE services/contracts does this program consume rather than rebuild?
2. What domain-specific truth does it own?
3. What truth/authority is explicitly outside its ownership?
4. Which canonical OCE objects cross its boundary?
5. Which operational block(s) ultimately host its reusable surfaces?
6. Which evidence proves a capability rather than merely describing one?
7. How does failure become evidence/NegativeKnowledge?
8. What is the minimum safe implementation sequence?
9. What are the independent review / reproducibility gates?
10. What is the promotion path into OCE, if any?

A book that cannot answer these is not ready to become a build prompt.
