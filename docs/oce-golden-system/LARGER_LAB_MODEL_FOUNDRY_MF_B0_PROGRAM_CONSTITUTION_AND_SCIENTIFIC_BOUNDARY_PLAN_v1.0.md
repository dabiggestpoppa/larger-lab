# 0. Block contract

MF-B0 answers one question before any serious dataset/model/GPU work:

> **What is the Model Foundry allowed to be?**

It does not train a useful model.
It does not choose the winning base model.
It does not choose the permanent GPU provider.
It does not build a second workflow engine.
It does not certify a runtime for OCE.

It freezes the boundaries that make later experimentation scientifically interpretable and institutionally safe.

MF-B0 is therefore successful when future agents can innovate aggressively **without having to improvise the Foundry's identity, evidence rules, or authority boundaries**.

# 2. Chapter 2 — Scientific Truth and Evidence Grammar

## MF-B0.C2.S1 — Observation/claim/evidence separation

### Contract

Freeze the Foundry truth grammar:

```text
OUTPUT / MEASUREMENT
        ↓
OBSERVATION
        ↓
EVIDENCE after provenance/applicability validation
        ↓
CLAIM ASSESSMENT
        ↓
DOMAIN CAPABILITY CANDIDATE
        ↓
OCE REVIEW if institutional promotion is requested
```

### Forbidden shortcuts

- score → capability truth;
- model prose → evidence;
- benchmark win → production readiness;
- paper claim → architecture fact;
- PnL → scientific validity.

### Gate

Fixtures prove each forbidden shortcut fails closed.

---

## MF-B0.C2.S2 — Evaluation tier constitution

### Contract

Ratify evaluation roles:

- DEVELOPMENT;
- PROMOTION;
- SEALED_CONFIRMATION.

### Required rules

- Development results may influence training.
- Promotion exposure is counted.
- Sealed evaluation is sparse and governed.
- No result can upgrade its own evaluation tier.
- Compromised/saturated/stale benchmarks can be demoted/retired.

### Gate

A public benchmark with many development exposures cannot be relabeled SEALED without a new protected task set/version.

---

## MF-B0.C2.S3 — Capability vector and hard floors

### Contract

Capability remains multidimensional.

### Minimum initial axes

- correctness;
- coding/tool execution;
- calibration;
- abstention;
- evidence discipline;
- research-method correctness;
- fabrication/hallucination;
- context efficiency;
- latency/cost;
- robustness;
- recovery;
- operator burden.

### Rule

Role-specific hard floors may be noncompensable.

No universal Foundry score becomes canonical truth.

### Gate

Two equal-average candidates with different catastrophic weaknesses remain distinguishable.

---

## MF-B0.C2.S4 — Uncertainty and inconclusive states

### Contract

The Foundry may legally conclude:

- UNDERPOWERED;
- INCONCLUSIVE;
- DATA_BLOCKED;
- COMPUTE_BLOCKED;
- RIGHTS_BLOCKED;
- CONTAMINATED;
- PROTOCOL_VIOLATION.

### Gate

Insufficient compute/sample/evidence can never be silently converted to PASS/FAIL solely to finish a workflow.

---

## MF-B0.C2.S5 — Negative-result value

### Contract

A result is successful if it materially reduces uncertainty or closes an EvidenceGap, even when the tested model/architecture loses.

### Required telemetry

Track:

- EvidenceGap closed;
- uncertainty reduced;
- invalid path eliminated;
- reusable method/test created;
- cost;
- reopen condition.

### Gate

Progress reports cannot use training-run count or parameter count as the primary evidence of institutional progress.

# 3. Chapter 3 — Data, Rights, and Contamination Constitution

## MF-B0.C3.S1 — Source role state machine

### Contract

Every source begins untrusted/unassigned and receives explicit role disposition.

Target roles:

- TRAIN;
- DEV_EVAL;
- PROMOTION_EVAL;
- SEALED_CONFIRMATION;
- BLIND_DISCOVERY;
- RETRIEVAL_ONLY;
- QUARANTINED;
- EXCLUDED.

### Required semantics

- role assignment has actor/provenance;
- role changes are versioned;
- derived artifacts inherit ancestor restrictions by default;
- unknown role never implies TRAIN.

### Gate

Retrieval-only content summarized by a model remains non-trainable until explicit governed role change.

---

## MF-B0.C3.S2 — Rights disposition

### Contract

Separate source facts from policy permission.

Required states:

- RIGHTS_VERIFIED_BY_POLICY;
- RIGHTS_RESTRICTED;
- RIGHTS_UNKNOWN;
- REVIEW_REQUIRED;
- EXCLUDED_BY_POLICY.

### Gate

No missing/ambiguous license becomes training authorization automatically.

---

## MF-B0.C3.S3 — Contamination taxonomy

### Contract

Adopt typed contamination evidence rather than CLEAN/DIRTY simplification.

Minimum classes:

- C0 no observed overlap;
- C1 source-family proximity;
- C2 semantic/task proximity;
- C3 paraphrase/solution overlap;
- C4 direct item/answer overlap;
- C5 upstream provenance unknown.

### Gate

C0 + C5 must remain distinguishable from a claim of proven cleanliness.

# 5. Chapter 5 — Authority, Resource, and Economic Boundary

## MF-B0.C5.S1 — Authority ceiling

### Contract

Model Foundry has no authority to:

- deploy production OCE runtimes;
- grant itself broader OCE tools;
- change OCE Constitution;
- place trades;
- connect live broker/exchange execution by default;
- approve its own evaluator changes;
- certify its own consequence-bearing runtime.

### Gate

A model becoming more capable does not change its grants.

---

## MF-B0.C5.S2 — Research budget semantics

### Contract

Budget is configurable `ResourceBudget`, not architecture constant.

Current operator intent such as $10–30/week is a live budget configuration, not permanent law.

### Required resource dimensions

- dollars;
- accelerator hours;
- storage;
- network/egress;
- time;
- model/API usage;
- operator interruptions.

### Gate

Budget exhaustion produces hold/underpowered state, not unauthorized spend.

---

## MF-B0.C5.S3 — Compute-provider neutrality

### Contract

Foundry expresses `ComputeRequest`; provider selection is replaceable.

Providers such as Octa/RunPod/Colab/Vast are implementations only.

### Gate

Experiment semantics remain reconstructable when provider identity changes.

---

## MF-B0.C5.S4 — Cost-to-close without scalar capture

### Contract

Resource routing preserves vector:

- $/hr;
- wall time;
- throughput;
- failure probability;
- restart cost;
- storage/network;
- security/trust;
- total realized cost.

Task-specific routing may select an implementation, but no universal cheapest-provider scalar becomes truth.

### Gate

A higher $/hr provider may be selected when expected total experiment cost/reliability is superior.

---

## MF-B0.C5.S5 — Sunk-cost firewall

### Contract

Past spend cannot weaken future evidence or stopping requirements.

### Gate

Identical weak scientific evidence yields the same stop/continue judgment whether historical spend was $1 or $1,000, absent a new explicit economic objective.

# 16. Final block doctrine

MF-B0 should make later innovation freer, not slower.

The purpose of the boundary is not to prescribe today's model stack forever.

It is to ensure that when models, architectures, datasets, accelerators, training methods, and providers change, Larger Lab never has to rediscover:

- what counts as evidence;
- who owns authority;
- how contamination is represented;
- how results remain reproducible;
- how negative results remain useful;
- how doctrine stays challengeable;
- how research systems remain replaceable;
- or how a model remains an organ rather than becoming the institution.

> **Freeze the scientific constitution so the technology can move fast.**
