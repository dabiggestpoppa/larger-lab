TIER A — TIGHT BOUNDARY (Shallow float confirmed, 23.6-38.2% partial rebalancing stall):
  Condition: Partial rebalancing stalls at 38.2% or 23.6% of initial extension leg
  Boundary placement: AT Asian High (bull) or Asian Low (bear) — 0-2p buffer
  Logic: 38.2% stall holds outside band with avg 0.8p precision.
         Any M5 candle CLOSE back inside band = 81% chance of full reversal → exit.
  Typical boundary distance from activation: initial_ext + 2p (usually 8-18p)
TIER B — STANDARD BOUNDARY (Moderate stall, 50-61.8% of initial leg):
  Condition: Partial rebalancing to 50% or 61.8% of leg, still above constraint band
  Boundary placement: 5-8p below Asian High (bull) / above Asian Low (bear)
  Logic: Median buffer on float days = 8.4p. Covers 82% of float days.
  Breakeven trigger: move to breakeven when continuation reaches 20p from band
TIER C — WIDE BOUNDARY (Deep stall, 78.6% of leg, approaching constraint band):
  Condition: Partial rebalancing reaches 78.6% of initial extension leg
  Boundary placement: AT Asian High/Low exactly (0p buffer — exit on M5 close inside)
  Logic: 78.6% stall holds with 1.0p avg. Close inside band = invalidation.
INVALIDATION RULE (all tiers):
  M5 CANDLE CLOSE back INSIDE the Asian constraint band = EXIT immediately.
  81.2% of band re-entries continue through the full constraint range.
  This is the structural invalidation signal, not a wick touch.
